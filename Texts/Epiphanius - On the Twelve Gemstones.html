St. Epiphanius — On the Twelve Gemstones

Full text

Prologue

To my noble lord, brother and fellow servant, Diodorus, the venerable bishop, from Epiphanius.

Rejoice in the Lord!

The Lord, who himself is the source, when he asked the Samaritan woman for water to drink, gave more grace than he received. And he sat at the well to recover from the weariness of the journey. He came in the body, so that the sequence of what had been said about the truth would not be revealed too soon. He who created human nature from himself and at the same time carried it within himself, fills everyone with excessive gifts. And by truly promising his care, he truly cares for everyone.

And when people turned to his disciples and servants with any requests, they gave them more than they received from them. Elijah, who spoke in this way beforehand, was like his Lord. And in his need he duly implored grace. But he went beyond his need and showed the widow who lived there abundant blessing. And she asked for bread, but he gave her the blessing in abundance beyond what was necessarily requested.

Similarly, it is with what your reverence demands from our lowliness: We receive more than we give. For in unceasing prayers to God we are closely bound to you, while the gift of grace overflows, although the lowliness does not diminish. And the vessel began to overflow from neediness toward freedom from need.

For you, O venerable one, asked from the beginning in your letter to write about how the stones were called with which the breastplates to be fastened on the shoulders of the high priests and on the breast of Aaron were adorned, and what color they displayed, together with the names and colors of the stones woven into them. Also about their appearance and where they are found. And about the nature of the stones that were brought for divine worship. And also about which stone was intended for which tribe, either where it was found or in which part of the land.

About the poverty of the widow we were completely ignorant until the miracle happened to her through the blessing. And to the Samaritan woman, instead of the sensually perceptible water, the gifts of grace of the mysteries of the Lord were revealed. The same happened to us. Although we too were lowly in senses, thinking, knowledge and words, there was abundance on all sides. Just as the great and abundant rains flowed through the prayers of Elijah and other prophets, whose son and disciple and representative you are, O venerable one! And likewise we too receive grace from the Lord like these and join you with our prayers.

Accordingly, we begin to speak about it, but first request gracious indulgence from you, and we want to strive, although we are weak, to answer your concern adequately.

The Twelve Stones and Their Arrangement

Earlier they were set in four rows. For the breastplate was divided into four parts and was itself square: a hand's breadth in length and in width according to the measure of the length. And at the beginning is Sardion, the first stone, then Topazion, then Emerald; and in the second row the first stone is Ruby, then Sapphire, and then Jasper; and in the third row the first stone is Ligyrion, then Agate, and then Amethyst; and in the fourth row, which is the last, the first stone is Chrysolite, then Beryl, then Onyx. And these are the twelve stones that were placed on the breastplate hanging from the shoulders; this is their selection and arrangement.

The First Stone: Sardion (Reuben's Tribe)

Sardion, the Babylonian: so named because it is fiery and blood-like in appearance. Sardion: it resembles a pickled, cut-open fish, which is why it was also called Sardion. It received its naming from its appearance. However, it is found in Babylon near Assyria. The stone is also transparent. In terms of power it is healing, because it is used for healing against swellings and ulcers when one anoints them. Also for other wounds that are cut by the sword.

Sardachat (source passage 1)
Sardonyx (source passage 1)

There is also another, Sardonyx, which is called Molochas; it alleviates the disease Steatomat. But looking through it, the yellowish appears less large than it is, especially during spring growth, when it is the beginning of Easter. There is also another, Sardachat, upon which looking a white round line appears. Like also Agate, about which the myth-makers said that it is a driver away of evil things. Therefore one also calls it peace-making.

The Second Stone: Topazion (Simeon's Tribe)

Topazion: It is reddish in appearance and resembles the stone called Ruby. But it was found among the Indians in the city of Topaz, who at a certain time cut stones there and found it in the middle of the stone. And it appeared to the stonemasons as splendid. And they rejoiced and showed it to some alabaster merchants and sold it at a low price. However, the alabaster merchants sold it at a high price to the Thebans. The Thebans in turn offered it to the queen who ruled at that time. And she set it on her crown and bound it to the middle of her forehead.

But this stone has the following effect: When a physician grinds the stone, it gives off no reddish water and does not drip according to its color; even when water collects in a large bowl, as much as the grinder wants. Even when so much dripped material accumulates, the stone does not decrease in its weight, nor does the measure of the stone become smaller in its appearance.

Moreover, what flows from it is useful for diseases of the eye. It is also effective against dropsy when someone drinks it. And also for those who suffer from seasickness. For physicians give the liquid obtained from the stone to children as medicine to drink.

The Third Stone: Emerald (Levi's Tribe)

Emerald, which is also the Green one, is yellowish in appearance. Between its types there are differences, for one of them is called Neronianos, the other Dometianos. It is bitter, very yellowish in appearance, and brightly transparent. But it is called Neronianos, as one says, for this reason: They say the mountain was soaked with the oil of King Nero, which is Spanish yellow or libertine, although others say: almond-colored. But others say susinian oil soaked the mountain. But others say: No.

Rather, they say the oil was filled in metian copper vessels and given to the Nicaeans. And from the copper rust the oil became yellowish over time and thus nourished the mountain. And as the oil was absorbed by the rock, it bloomed ever more in yellowishness and transparency. And others say: No. Rather, Neron was the name of a certain pinaropic artist, or a jeweler, who knew which of the emeralds is the noblest. And accordingly one calls it "Neronianos." That means: "new in splendor."

For Dometianus ruled as king; he was the son of Vespasianus, who conquered Jerusalem; who in turn was the brother of Titus. And Nero and Dometianus were selfish-proud kings.

There are also other emeralds: One is that which is found among the Indians and is very similar to Neronianos. The other was found in Ethiopia, which is near the river Phison. Therefore Holy Scripture sings, saying that it is found near the river Phison: "Phison is the one that covers the whole land of Evilat." For there lies the Green Stone. It is also said of the Ruby that it is from there.

And this river Phison is called 'Indian river' according to the language of the Greeks; but according to the barbarian peoples it is called 'Ganges' among the Evilaeans and Brachmans, among the Geziners and Bugaeans. For many of the Greek historians also know the river Ganges, which flows through the whole land of Evilat, by that name. But 'Evilaeans' the natives call the Indians. And flowing through it reaches toward evening the land called Ethiopia and empties into the ocean. Therefore they also say that the ruby stone was found at the borders of this river, which flows into the lands of the Libyans, where in earlier times those lived who were called Ethiopians.

And Aquila translated the green stone that is in Genesis neither as Green Stone nor as Emerald, but in the following way through use of the Hebrew translation. And finding somewhere the Hebrew designation of this stone, he translated it as Bodali, instead of the corresponding Jewish Green Stone. Just as one also says, "there was the Green Stone." Aquila says, "there was the Bdelion." But there is Bdelion, either the anointing oil or the incense that is brought from India.

But the varieties of emeralds are eight that are found of these. And the nature of these is beautifully colored, intense and soft colored. They are from the lands of the Indians and Ethiopians.

But now it is necessary that we devote ourselves in the following to the mountain, which as said was watered for a time either by Nero or by Dometianus or by those who are in the land. But it is inside at the so-called Red Sea, near the entrance to the land of the Indians.

And among the Indians there is great diversity of it. For once nine kings ruled according to the historical account over the Alabastrians, Homeritans, Axometans together with Adulitans, Tanisnaziians, Asabenoneons, Dirnonians and Helenkibenonians. But now there are also many more divisions: For earlier the Dibeners were one with the Fish-eaters, and the Sirindibeners with the Zakamtazins, and the Elendibeners with the Evilaeans. And they were from other places, as we noted following the historians.

But the mountains about which our speech is are under the rule of the Romans. The so-called Emerald Island by nature is located opposite Beronike, the port of India, whose face lies toward the broadly laid out Hebrew Thebaida. It lies at a distance from the sea like the journey of a messenger ship, that is eighty miles. It is located near Beronike and is also very near Elephantine and Pelmi. But today Blemmyers rule over it. But the quarries of this mountain; in the barbarian land among the same Blemmyers near the settlements, from whose mountains today the barbarians break out the emerald.

But this is the power of this stone Emerald: If you look into it, you see your face as in a mirror. And the myth-makers say that it has the power of foresight.

The Fourth Stone: Ruby (Judah's Tribe)

The Ruby, whose appearance is strongly red. It is found in Karchedon of the Libyans, whose name is Africa. But some say that this stone was found as follows: During the day one did not see it, but at night it was perceived from afar, similar to a lamp. Or the Ruby was perceived because of its sparkling nature. And there is an hour when this Ruby shines, and an hour when its rays cease to sparkle. Whereby this appears puzzling to those who observe it from afar; for it is sometimes seen, and sometimes it ceases: But through this they recognize that this is this stone. Following the shining sparks of this stone, one finds it. But whoever possesses it has no possibility to hide it. Even if he tries to hide it through many clothes, it will be seen through the clothing because of its shining sparkle. Consequently it was called "Sparks" for these reasons.

And this one has a slight resemblance to the stone Keraunios. Some call it 'wine-like Keronion.' Or it is also called Karchedonios because in its appearance it is similar to this stone. For it was found like this one in the same place. But it is not delightful.

And Holy Scripture says that this comes from the river Phison, although it is also to be found in Africa. For the river Phison flows into the sea, from the southern side toward the western ocean through all places. And as the stone falls from the western side into the river Phison and foams up here, one sees it from the side of the Libyans.

But Holy Scripture says: "A river flows from Eden, and from there it divides into four parts. One is called Phison. This is the one that surrounds the whole land of Evilat, where gold is. The gold of this land is good. There is the stone Ruby and the Green one."

The Fifth Stone: Sapphire (Dan's Tribe)

The stone called Sapphire is purple-colored. Like gold brocade, in appearance with black-purple shades. Its types are also many: For there is of this a royal one, whose row is densely golden. But it cannot delight as much as the one that is purple-colored in its entirety. And this was found among the Ethiopian Indians. Therefore one says that the 365 steps of the Dionysus temple, which is among the Indians, are completely made of sapphire stone, although this matter is incredible. But one says that each individual of the steps is made of sapphire stones, according to their respective size. One has often also set it on outer garments and rings, especially the kings. For it is a wonderful stone, beautiful and delightful in appearance. And therefore some have called it Homisitos.

It is also disease-repelling. For if someone grinds it and mixes it with milk, and thereby anoints the infected places, the itching and fungal diseases heal. It is also written in the law of Moses: The face that he saw in the mountains appeared on the sapphire stone.

The Sixth Stone: Jasper (Naphtali's Tribe)

The stone Jasper is emerald-colored in its appearance, but is found at the edge of the mouth of the river Thermodontos and also near Amatunt. Not that which is in Cyprus, but a naturally large type that is called Amatheusion. But its appearance is as follows: According to the appearance of emerald it is yellowish, but opaque-foggy, pure-yellow; and in its core it resembles copper. But one delights in it as if one were out of one's senses, as the myths say.

Another type is rough and its color is of darker shading than the flowers of the sea. And there is also another type that is found in the grottoes of the Phrygian mountains, whose name is Ide, similar to a blood-red shell: colorfully transparent and more wine-like and more golden-yellow than amethyst.

For it does not correspond to one color and is not the same in effect, but one is smoky, and whiter than the smoke in the air, and extremely luminous. But its bluish glow does not disappear again. Similar to how from the winter-cold earth the water vapor rises, as soon as the south wind ceases. The other type resembles ice, and as the myth-makers say, it is a remedy for fever fantasies. This type has been found among the Iberians and among the Hyrcanians; and among those who rule over the land of the Caspians. And another type of Jasper – not very luminous – is yellow and has lines in the middle.

And another type of Jasper, which is called Pallios, resembles snow or sea foam. Therefore the mythmakers also say that the animals in the fields – and even the monsters – fear it.

The Seventh Stone: Ligyrion (Gad's Tribe)

About the finding of Ligyrion we could quite simply find out nothing. Neither from the sources, nor from earlier testimonies, and also not from those who have dealt with it. However, we found Lungurion, a so-called stone, which some in the commonly known languages call Lingurion. Therefore I thought that this is Ligyrion, because in Holy Scripture the names are often given in changed form. For thus Scripture sometimes calls the Emerald the Green Stone, and names others with other names.

Either Lingoros or also Lanouros, if this is supposed to be Ligurion. I say once again that its location has not become known to us, however I have come to the understanding that it comes from the Upper Thebans. Therefore one says, if these are those stones, they are from such stones from the temple of Asclepius, from the so-called Memphis of the Egyptians. And in this stone there are inclusions, not similar to serpentine, but it has points in similarity to the 'wax-Kerinon' called stone. And its points are yellowish; so that we must recognize that in the middle of the depth of this rock there is a vein that is much nobler and brighter than this stone.

It is especially beautiful, more than the rock from which it received its naming. And it is translated as Lingoros because there is a certain animal that is called Lingion, whose color is similar to the ox calf or the Red Heifer. And its tail is short and has a multitude of yellowish points. Therefore its name was called Lunguros, which means 'tail of Liungos.'

Hyacinth and its Scythian varieties

Ligyrion (source passage 1)

But I am extremely impressed that Holy Scripture also concerned itself with the decorations and the glorious beauty of royal crowns, and with those of priestly garments, to which the noble stones have given such adornment. But many other and unknown noble stones are also mentioned: I speak about the Ruby and the Emerald, about Amethyst and Agate, and about Beryl and Chrysolite. Although the stones are not mentioned by these names, neither is the Hyacinth, which is an important, noble, and glorious stone. Therefore, it is necessary to consider whether perhaps Holy Scripture calls this stone Ligyrion.

For the color appearances of hyacinth are manifold. And the darker the color of hyacinth is found, the nobler it is than other hyacinths. But it resembles a bluish sheep's wool cloth or also the sea-green with slightly purple shades; therefore Holy Scripture also says: With bluish and with purple the priestly garments were adorned.

The first stone is called Thalassites; and yet another stone is called Rhodiaios, and yet another Natibos. And yet another stone is more water-colored than this, which is called Chauniaios; and yet another is called Pallios, and yet another Perileukios. But these are found in the interior of the barbarians among the Scythians.

And as Scythia the northern directions are usually designated by the old writers: Where the Gundes are and the Vetens, and the Gonkes and Venens, and Ariosans and Arianosans; up to the Germans and Amazons, which translated means "Land of Women." And there in the interior of the desert is on a rocky height near great Scythia a deep gorge that is inaccessible to humans: The rocks are surrounded by clouds on this side and that, so that from above, from the summit of the mountains, looking down as onto a wall one cannot see the bottom of the gorge. Because of the deepest depth it is darkened like an abyss.

And those who were called by the kings there to find these stones were brought by force because they were condemned to death. And these, by killing and slaughtering sheep, scattered their meat from above from the rocks into the depths of the abyss. And the stones were, as one says, sacrificed together with the limbs of the slaughtered sheep. And when then the eagles that lived on the summits of the rocks ate the meat, the stones remained on the summits of the rocks, and one sent those condemned to death to the stones. And these, thanks to their observational ability, watching where the eagles brought the meat of the sheep, went thus and found the stones that had remained on the rocks. And they took and brought them to the clients.

But all these stones, whatever color they have, are after all valuable and extremely costly. And they have the following effect: If one throws them into a strongly blazing fire, they take no damage from it, rather they extinguish the fire. Not only that, but also if one takes these stones and wraps them in a cloth, or in another linen fabric, and with this linen lays them over the sparks, the same one who holds the wrapped thing in his hand will not endure the heat and will lament painfully from burning. However, the cloth in which the stone is wrapped and laid into the fire will take absolutely no damage and not even singe.

About this stone it is also said that it is useful for women in childbirth and eases the birth. It also drives away nightmares in the same way.

The Eighth Stone: Agate (Asher's Tribe)

It has been supposed that the name of the Agate stone comes from Perileukios, which is found according to the similarity of the hyacinth stone. Glorious in its appearance it is bluish, having a white outer mantle, according to the appearance of hooves or also ivory. And the same is also found in the dwelling places of the Scythians. Among these stones, however, agate is found as one that has the color of a lion: Ground however with water, anointed on the places, it wards off poisons that come from viper and scorpion bites and the like. But this I say about the lion-like agate.

The Ninth Stone: Amethyst (Issachar's Tribe)

Amethyst is flaming according to its edge, dense. But its glow is radiantly white and inside the body it shows wine-like appearance. But different is its form of appearance in the mountains. And I also think that it was found among the Libyans. For one is similar to pure hyacinth, the other to Amphikochlon. But this type is found on the shores of the sea of the Libyans. And the myth-makers say about this that it would indicate in advance the southern winds, winter and rainfalls.

The Tenth Stone: Chrysolite (Zebulun's Tribe)

Topaz (source passage 1)

The stone Chrysolite, which some have called Chrysoberyl, is gold-colored. It is found at the water well on the shore of Babylon at the Achesenidic rock. This water well is called Babylonian and the rock Achesenidic. For they say that Achaimeneos, the father of King Cyrus, called it thus. As some historians have transmitted. There is also a gold-green one against stomach pains and diarrhea, which ground and drunk has a healing effect.

The Eleventh Stone: Beryl (Joseph's Tribe)

The stone Beryl is opaque, sea-colored, air-like to amethyst and has the appearance like Paiderot and water-colored hyacinth. This is from the volcanic eruptions of the mountain that is called Tauros. If someone wants to turn it back and forth under the sun, luminous, glassy points like millet appear in its middle. And another Beryl is similar to the pupil of a dragon. And yet another Beryl is similar to white wax; and yet another Beryl to something frozen. But this Beryl is found beyond the river Euphrates, at Tauros.

The Twelfth Stone: Onyx (Benjamin's Tribe)

The stone Onyx has a distinctly golden-yellow color. At this stone – as one says – the brides of kings and the daughters of the great lords delighted. Those who have these stones delight in them to the point of self-forgetfulness. But there also exist other stones similar in name to Beryl, which are honey-colored like wax; some say that they would be congealed from water drops. But Beryl is – those who investigate the name most truthfully say this – from the appearance of the nail of agile people, because its shimmer is similar to the nail, together with blood-red appearance. But the marble stone is also unnecessarily and falsely designated with the name Onyx because of its pure whiteness.

These therefore are the twelve stones that one had commanded at the time to place on the shoulder of the priests, and about these stones we have reported according to our weakness from very different testimonies, to the extent that we were able to find out about their properties and locations. And we have also noted their powers briefly for now, proceeding from the naming of the respective stone, as it has fitted together in logical sequence.

Theological Interpretations

Now the question arises for us how we appropriately and fittingly explain the arrangement of the sequence that follows. For one has commanded to establish twelve stones according to the twelve tribes, so that one stone is due to each tribe. And to each individual name of each individual tribe one stone has been correspondingly assigned. Although the command was known which stone was intended for which tribe, and also which tribal name this stone should bear; this was easy for us, and it was also no question for us to find out the determination of the sequence. But about the report of the powers of the stones we are also concerned in the following.

But now it has not been defined, but only in two places do we find the sequence of these stones and the nature of their arrangement. Just as we also find it in the breastplate that was placed in the middle on the breast of the priest. And on his shoulder, on his arms, were two golden rings. The rings on the hems, fastened on the sides, held the breastplate in their middle and connected again with the upper rings that were placed on each shoulder.

For he had on his shoulders buttons connecting one hem with the other made of gold-set stones. These were placed at the ends, one on each shoulder: one on the right side, the other on the left. Bent forward the rings reached the collarbone of the priest, as if the rings of the breastplate were interwoven and connected with themselves through two rings that hung down from the shoulders, where also were the golden plates that were on his shoulders.

But this shoulder plate, as I have already said, had twelve stones, woven in order and arranged in four rows, which were adapted to each other, as their order prescribed it. The first row, which is also the uppermost, had from the left side of the priest toward the right, the stones as follows: Sardion, Topazion, Emerald.

For it is necessary to explain this too: from where their arrangement began; and also where it in turn ended. But it is known that according to the actual Hebrew characters this was the arrangement. For the Hebrews also write this arrangement, drawing from left to right and subordinating one to the other. Although they themselves hold the following hand in the letters from right to left. But there were also arrangements on the breast of the priest drawing from left to right.

But that was, as I already said, the first row: Sardion, Topazion and Emerald. And the second: Ruby, Sapphire and Jasper. And the third: Ligyrion, Agate and Amethyst. And the fourth, which is also the last: Chrysolite, Beryl and Onyx.

And again we have found the same stones placed in the middle on both shoulders: and not from different stones, but only from Emerald. And the names of the sons of Israel were written and engraved according to the words of the Lord's command.

But again it is not certainly known which name was ordered to be engraved on the right side on the Green Stone, or which name was decided to be arranged again on the left side on the stone Emerald.

But it is quite rightly known to us that there were six names of the sons of Israel on the right side and six on the left. For the one stone could not bear the names of the twelve tribes because of its roundness. But also not, as I said, could it place double twelve tribes on the right side, and also not on the left side.

For it is only said: "The names of the sons of Israel." Thus the reading cited below also has for what was said, which we have also assigned to the same words.
