Diamond

The diamond is an exceedingly hard, bright stone, commonly small and crystal-clear or iron-coloured. ¹ ² ³ ⁴ ⁵ ⁶ ⁷ ⁹ It governs iron and the magnet, resists poison and sorcery, quiets fear and madness, and protects its bearer in contests and danger. ⁷ ⁸ The methods by which it may be cut or broken differ by kind.

Recorded localities: India, Arabia, Cyprus, Syria, Macedonia, Ethiopia, and Philippi. ¹ ² ⁴ ⁵ ⁶ ⁷ ⁹ ¹⁰ ¹¹

Magnet

The magnet is iron-colored, or sometimes blue, and draws iron to itself, passing its power to the iron so that rings or needles may hang one from another. ² ⁷ It may guide a floating needle and compass toward the heavens, but garlic and Diamond oppose its attraction. ⁵ Certain kinds repel iron, attract human flesh, or are themselves drawn to iron. ¹ ³ ⁵

Recorded localities: India and the Indian Ocean, the country of the Troglodytes, Franconia, Ethiopia, Macedonia, Antioch, Alexandria, and Asia. ¹ ² ⁵ ⁸ ⁹ ¹⁰

Peantes

Peantes, also called Peantides, is an icy-colored stone found in Macedonia. ¹ It is spoken of as female: an older account says that it conceives at certain times and gives birth to a like stone. ¹ Another reading rejects this as literal, taking the stone’s feminine nature to mean that its virtues aid women in conceiving and giving birth; the matter is left open to judgment.

Recorded localities: Macedonia. ¹

Peranites

Peranites is a stone found in Macedonia, said to be of the female sex: in its season it conceives and gives birth to a natural stone like itself. ¹ It is held to benefit pregnant women. ¹

Recorded localities: Macedonia. ¹ ²

Poeanites

Poeanites is found in the mountains of Macedonia. ¹ Within the mine it heaves with motions like the pangs of childbirth, as though the earth conceived and brought forth a stone. ¹ Pregnant women trust in its protection at the dangerous moment of delivery.

Recorded localities: The mountains of Macedonia. ¹
