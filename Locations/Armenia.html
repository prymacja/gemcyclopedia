Amethyst

Amethyst is a purple or violet stone with shades from deep wine-colour to pale rose. ¹ ² ³ ⁴ ⁵ ⁶ It checks drunkenness and evil thoughts, strengthens understanding, protects against poison and serpents, and bears virtues in medicine, hunting, warfare, and engraved images. ⁶

Recorded localities: India, Libya and its coast, Arabia, Cyprus, Egypt, Armenia, Galatia, and Tarsus. ¹ ² ³ ⁵ ⁶ ⁷ ⁸

Bolus Armenus

Bolus Armenus, also called Ramai, is a reddish earth associated with Armenia. ¹ ² Though it is counted among stones for its virtues, it is not itself a stone. ² It is cold and dry, and is used against bowel fluxes, fever, dysentery, menstrual bleeding, bloody expectoration, and disorders of the spleen and stomach.

Recorded localities: A vein of earth in Armenia. ² ³

Vernix

Vernix, also called Armenian Stone, is pale and remedies melancholia or black bile, disorders of the spleen and liver, and heart ailments. ¹ ²

Recorded localities: One account associates the name with Armenia. ²
