Amber

Amber, also called Succinus, Succinum, Electrum, Sucinus, Lambra, Harpaga, and Karabe, is a yellow tree gum that hardens into a gem. ¹ ² ³ It may be clear as glass or tawny like wax, sometimes with blue and saffron hues. It attracts light objects when warmed by rubbing, and its smoke drives away serpents and other poisonous creatures. ³

Recorded localities: Dacia, England, Brittany, and especially along the Northern Ocean opposite Gessaria Island. ³ Amber hardened on shore-side trees may fall into the sea and later be cast onto the coast. ⁴ ⁵

Pontica

Pontica is a transparent, sea-blue gem from the Pontus, or Black Sea. ¹ ² ³ It bears red drops or stars, golden marks, or long red lines. ³ By this stone a person may speak with demons, drive them away, and compel them to answer. ²

Recorded localities: The Pontus, or Black Sea. ¹ ³

Trachius

Trachius, spelled Thracius in another account, is black and resonant. another account also records an almost green kind that is not transparent. ² The black kind is found in the river of Equitia called Pontus. ¹

Recorded localities: The river of Equitia called Pontus. ¹
