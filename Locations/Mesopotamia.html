Beryl

Beryl, also called Beryllus or Berillus, has many kinds, from pale and transparent to opaque, sea-green, olive, or dark. ¹ ⁶ Some contain a moving, water-like light and shine when cut with six faces. ⁵ Beryl is found in India, Tauros, beyond the Euphrates, and Babylon. ² ³ ⁶ Its virtues include victory, concord, healing, and the expulsion of poison; ⁴ its use in sunlight and its many named varieties are set out below. ¹

Recorded localities: India; ¹ ³ Mount Tauros and lands beyond the Euphrates; ² Babylon. ⁶ ⁷ ⁸

Cimilanitus

Cimilanitus is marble-colored, with a golden or saffron pupil at its center, and is found in the Euphrates. ¹

Recorded localities: The Euphrates River. ¹

Mithridax

Mithridax, also called Mithrax or Mitridax, sparkles in many colors when struck by sunlight. ¹ ² It is found in Persia and is also associated with the Euphrates.

Recorded localities: Persia and by the Euphrates. ¹ ²

Zmilaces

Zmilaces, also called Zmilanthis, Zmilanthus, or Zmilampis, is a blue-mingled marble-like stone from the Euphrates, with a gray or blue-green eye-like center. ¹ ²

Recorded localities: The channel of the Euphrates. ¹ ²
