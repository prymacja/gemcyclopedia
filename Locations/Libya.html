Amethyst

Amethyst is a purple or violet stone with shades from deep wine-colour to pale rose. ¹ ² ³ ⁴ ⁵ ⁶ It checks drunkenness and evil thoughts, strengthens understanding, protects against poison and serpents, and bears virtues in medicine, hunting, warfare, and engraved images. ⁶

Recorded localities: India, Libya and its coast, Arabia, Cyprus, Egypt, Armenia, Galatia, and Tarsus. ¹ ² ³ ⁵ ⁶ ⁷ ⁸

Exacolitus

Exacolitus is a stone of mixed and varied colors. ¹ ² It has a dissolving virtue and, prepared with wine, is used against colic and internal pains.

Recorded localities: Libya, among the Troglodytes. ³

Haematite

Haematite, also called Hematite, Ematites, Emathitis, Emathites, Sanguineus Lapis, or Sedeneg, is a blood-veined, iron-colored stone that turns blood-red when ground. ³ ⁴ It preserves rather than corrodes the body and stops bleeding; ¹ its many remedies treat the eyes, wounds, discharges, snakebite, and bladder stones. ²

Recorded localities: Ethiopia, Arabia, Afriia, Egypt, Babylonia, Spain, Libya, Phrygia, and Germany. ¹ ² ³ ⁴ ⁵ ⁶

Heliotrope

Heliotrope, also called Eliotropia, Elitropia, Elitropus, or Bloodstone, is a green stone like Emerald, clouded and speckled with blood-red spots and veins. ¹ ² ⁴ Anointed with the juice of its namesake herb and set in water beneath the sun, it turns the sun blood-red, boils the water into mist, and brings rain. ¹ ³ It grants prophecy and long life, checks bleeding and poison, and, with its herb and secret rite, makes its bearer invisible. ¹ ³

Recorded localities: Ethiopia, Cyprus, Africa, India, and Libya; ¹ ² ³ ⁴ necromancers call it the Babylonian gem. ¹ ⁴ ⁵ ⁶

Hexacontalite

Hexacontalite, also called Hexaconta, Execonthalitus, or Hexecontalithos, is a small, multicolored stone containing the colors of sixty gems. ¹ ² ³ It is found in Libya among the Troglodytes, and its virtues match the colors it bears. ¹

Recorded localities: Libya among the Troglodytes. ¹ ² ³

Jet

Jet (Gagates, Kacabre) is usually black, smooth, and light, though it may also be yellow, citron-colored, or grey. ¹ ² ³ It is found in Sicily, Libya, Lycia, Britain, and along northern shores. ¹ It attracts straw when rubbed, burns with a strong odor, and its smoke and preparations are credited with many remedies and protections. A citron-colored kind is also called amber in one account; ⁴ this named kind is retained here as a form of jet, while Amber remains a separate article.

Recorded localities: Sicily and Britain; ² ⁴ also Libya, Lycia, England, and the northern coast of Teutonia. ¹ ⁵ ⁶

Liparea

Liparea, also called Lippares or Liparia, draws wild animals. ⁴ Burned, it calls them; ¹ carried in the woods, it brings game to a hunter’s spear without hounds or snares. ² Hunted beasts run to it for protection. ³ One account says the beasts cannot be seen by dogs or hunters while they gaze upon the stone, but marks this marvel as uncertain.

Recorded localities: Scythia and Libya. ² ³ ⁴ ⁵

Obsidian

Obsidian is a black or green translucent stone like glass. ¹ ² Polished, it reflects shadowy images and is used in mirrors and gems. ¹ ²

Recorded localities: India, Italy (including Samnium), Spain, Libya, and Germany. ¹ ²

Ruby

Ruby, also called Carbuncle, Anthrax, Rubinus, and Sparks, is a hard, clear red stone whose fiery rays shine like a coal in the darkness. ¹ ³ ⁵ ⁶ A noble ruby glows by night; ² ³ ⁴ its radiance can reveal it even through clothing. Libya and the land of the Troglodytes yield carbuncles, while other accounts place ruby in Karchedon and the river Phison. ²

Recorded localities: Libya, among the Troglodytes; ¹ ³ ⁶ Karchedon in Africa; ² India; and the river Phison. ² ⁷ ⁸

Sapphire

The sapphire shines in blue, azure, purple, and yellow forms. It cools heat and lust, heals the eyes and body, preserves health, and brings peace, devotion, and understanding. Prepared or engraved in particular ways, it also counters poison, bleeding, spirits, illness, and misfortune. ¹ ² ³ ⁴ ⁵ ⁶ ⁷ The name Sirites also appears in accounts of Silenite, whose lunar properties are treated separately.

Recorded localities: India, Ethiopia, Media, Libya, and near Le Puy in Provence. ¹ ² ³ ⁵ ⁶ ⁹ ¹⁰

Sixtystone

Sixtystone is a very small stone marked with sixty colors. ¹ It is found in Libya and among the Troglodytes, and is said to harm the nerves and make a man’s eyes tremble. ¹

Recorded localities: Libya and among the Troglodytes. ¹
