Astrion

Astrion, also called Asterites, Astrites, and Asterius, is a white Crystal-like stone with a star-shaped light enclosed within it. ³ It reflects white rays in sunlight; ¹ when held facing the stars, it catches their gleam and casts it back. ² Its central star shines like the full moon. It is found in India, Thrace, and Carmania.

Recorded localities: India; ² Thrace; ³ Carmania. ³ ⁴ ⁵
