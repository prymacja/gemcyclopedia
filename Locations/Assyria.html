Sardion

Sardion is a transparent, fiery, blood-red stone found in Babylon near Assyria. ¹ Its cut, fish-like appearance gives it its name. ¹ Applied by anointing, it heals swellings and ulcers, and it is also used for wounds made by the sword. ¹ It is a separate entry from Sard in the source tradition.

Recorded localities: Babylon near Assyria. ¹
