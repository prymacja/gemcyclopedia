Properties

Displayed, Limphicus frees a person from seizures. ¹ Wrapped in silk, it protects against infections of the eyes, jaw, and throat, and against coughs and headaches. ¹ This protection extends into the future as well as the present.

Sources

Open cited source

Limphicus is a stone of great virtue. Displayed, it frees [a person] from seizures. Wrapped in silk it protects against all infections of the eyes, jaw, and throat, and cough and headaches, not only in the present moment but for the future.
