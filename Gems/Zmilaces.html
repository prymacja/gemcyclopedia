Zmilaces

Zmilaces

FieldAccount
NamesZmilaces; ² Zmilanthis; ² Zmilanthus; ¹ Zmilampis. ¹
AppearanceLike marble, mingled with blue; its center is gray or blue-green and shines like an eye’s pupil. ¹ ²
Found inThe channel of the Euphrates. ¹ ²

Zmilaces, also called Zmilanthis, Zmilanthus, or Zmilampis, is a blue-mingled marble-like stone from the Euphrates, with a gray or blue-green eye-like center. ¹ ²

Generic

An eye-patterned agate with a gray-blue center, used as an analogue for Zmilaces’s pupil-like marking (source).

Zmilaces is a marble-like stone mixed with blue, found in the Euphrates. ¹ ² Its central pupil-like mark is described as blue-green or gray. ¹ The spellings Zmilanthis, Zmilanthus, and Zmilampis denote the same Euphrates stone in these accounts. ¹

Sources

  1. Zmilanthus (i.e. zmilampis) is collected in the channel of the Euphrates and is similar to Proconnesian marble, with a gray color in the middle shining like the pupil of the eye.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, “Zmilaces”

Open the cited text

Zmilaces or Zmilanthis is a stone the color of marble, mixed with blue. It is found in the Euphrates, and at its center it has a blue-green [or gray] colored pupil.
