Brontea

NamesBrontea. ¹
AppearanceIts shape or color is not described.
Found inIn the head of a turtle. ¹

Brontea is a stone from a turtle’s head, associated with thunder, and it counters lightning. ¹ Its name is linked to Greek βροντή (brontē), “thunder.” ¹

Generic

Description

Brontea is found in a turtle’s head and is associated with thunder. ¹ Its name recalls the Greek word βροντή (brontē), “thunder.” ¹

Properties

Brontea counters the lightning-bolt. ¹

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI

Open cited source

Brontea, from the head of the turtle, is thought to fall from thunder (cf. βροντή (brontē), “thunder”) and to counteract the lightning-bolt.
