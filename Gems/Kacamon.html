Generic

Description

Kacamon is often white, either throughout or in part, though its colors vary. It is most frequently found mixed with Onyx. ¹ No particular virtue is assigned to the uncarved stone; its power is attributed to the images and carvings on it and to its sigils.

Properties

The stone’s power is said to arise from the images and carvings upon it and from its sigils; ¹ their forms and effects are discussed elsewhere in the work.
