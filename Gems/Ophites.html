Ophites is a kind of Marble marked with serpentine spots. ¹ It has a soft white kind and a hard dark kind, the latter greenish and sprinkled with yellow spots. ¹ It is found in Germany and is worked into vessels, building decoration, and roofing planks. Hung by the neck, it heals headaches and serpent bites; ¹ its white kind restores the insane and lethargic. ¹

Generic

Description

Ophites is a kind of Marble bearing serpentine markings. It occurs in two forms: soft white stone and hard dark stone, the latter greenish with yellow spots. It hardens with fire. ¹

Properties

When Ophites is hung by the neck, it heals headaches and serpent bites. ¹ The soft white kind is believed to restore health to the insane and the lethargic. ¹

The ancients adorned building walls with Ophites. ¹ In Germany it is made into drinking vessels, and some believe it to be the stone from which cauldrons were made. ¹ Its softer kind can be cut and smoothed; ¹ in the Belgian provinces it is sawn into planks for roof tiles. ¹

Ophites is a type of marble, as said above under that heading [Marmor], which has serpentine spots. It is of two species: the soft and white, and the hard and black (or dark) with a certain greenish [tint] sprinkled with yellow spots. The ancients adorned the walls of buildings with it. When hung by the neck, it has the virtue of healing headaches and serpents’ bites. It is believed that the white kind restores to health the insane and the lethargic. It is found in Germany, where they make drinking vessels with it. Some think that ophites is that stone with which they made cauldrons. Because of its softness it may be cut and smoothed out. In the Belgian provinces they saw it into planks to make roof tiles for the homes. It hardens with fire.

The Ophitean is black and white with serpentine spots.
