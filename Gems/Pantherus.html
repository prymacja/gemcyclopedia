Pantherus

Pantherus

A colorful spotted leopard-skin jasper cabochon shown as an appearance analogue for Pantherus (source).

NamesPantherus; ¹ Pantheros. ²; Panteron ³; Pantera ⁴
AppearanceMany colors in one stone, including black, green, red, white, pale purple, and rose; ¹ its patterns resemble a panther’s spotted hide. ² ³ ⁴
Found inMostly Media. ¹ ³ ⁴

Pantherus, also called Pantheros, gathers many colors in one stone: black, red, green, white, purple, and rose. ¹ ² It is found mostly in Media and is named for the spotted panther’s hide. ² Its colors bring many powers: the stone is said to impair sight, while looking upon it at sunrise or daybreak brings success, victory, and protection from enemies for that day.

Generic

Description

Pantherus is variegated with many colors in a single stone. ¹ Black, red, green, white, purple, and rose hues meet in it, forming patterns like a panther’s spotted hide. ² It is found mostly in Media. ¹

Properties

Pantherus is said to impair the sight. ¹ It is said to possess as many powers as it has colors. If one looks upon the stone early in the morning as the sun rises, success and victory follow; for that whole day it protects against enemies and keeps them from prevailing. ²

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II, Pantherus

Open cited passage

Pantherus is a stone having many colours in a single stone, namely black, green, red, and many more; it is also found pale purple and rose-coloured. They say it impairs the sight. It is found mostly in Media. The wearer should look at it early in the morning when the sun is rising, in order to be successful and victorious. It is said to have as many powers as it has colours.

2. Marbodus of Rennes — De lapidibus, LI. Pantheros

Open cited stanza

In the Pantheros varying colours meet, Where black and red, and green and white compete: Here rosy light, there brilliant purples play, And blooms the gem with varying patterns gay. At dawn of day its potent beauties view So shall success thy doings still pursue, For all that day, defended by the charm, No foe shall e'er prevail to work thee harm. All travellers tell how 'midst far India's groves Beauteous in spotted hide the panther roves. How furious lions dread his piercing ciy And trembling at the sound in terror fly. Marked like the beast that can the lion tame The spotted gem obtains the self-same name.

3. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 80: Panteron

Open cited source

Panteron is a stone of diverse colours, sprong and distinguished, for it is seen black, red, green, pale, purple, yellow, & also bright green in colour. This stone makes a man bold and hardy, so that he shall not be overcome that day that he seethe this stone early at Sun rising, as it is said in Lapidario.

4. Thomas Cantimpre — Liber de natura rerum, Book XIV: “On Precious Stones”, Pantera — Panther-stone

Open cited source

Pantera is a stone named after the animal called the panther. It displays almost every colour. Anyone carrying it should look at it in his hand at earliest morning, as the sun rises, so that he may be victorious and successful in every undertaking. It calms rage and is found in India. It is said to have as many powers as it has colours.
