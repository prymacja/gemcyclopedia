Emery

The image shows Naxos emery rock (source).

Emery, also called smyris in one account, is a dark gray to black granular rock composed chiefly of corundum mixed with other minerals. It has a coarse, granular texture. ¹ A passage on Chalcedony says that the stone is pierced with emery before being worn at the neck. ²

Chalcedonius (chalcedony) is a pale grey, or rather dark-coloured stone. If it is pierced by the power of the stone called smyris (emery) and hung around the neck, it is said to be good against fanciful illusions arising from melancholy.
