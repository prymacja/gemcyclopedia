Daemonius, also called Diamon or Daimon, is a double-colored stone like a rainbow and is named the Demon’s Bow. ¹ ² It heals fever, expels poison, and makes its bearer safe from enemies and victorious against them. ¹

Generic

Description

Daemonius is a double-colored stone that resembles a rainbow. ¹ It is also called Iris, from the rainbow, or the Demon’s Bow. ¹ ² Diamon and Daimon are other forms of its name, explained as “stone of the daimon.” ²

Properties

Daemonius is prescribed against fever and expels poison. ¹ ² It makes its bearer safe from enemies and victorious against them.

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Daemonius is a double-colored stone, like the rainbow, which is called iris, from which it takes its name, and is also called the demon’s bow. They say that its virtue is useful against fever, expels poisons, and renders its wearer safe from and victorious against the enemy.

2. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open cited source

Diamon daimon is reported to be named 'stone of the daimon', being of two colours like the rainbow, which is called 'bow of the daimon'. They say that it is prescribed for those suffering from fever, and expels poison.
