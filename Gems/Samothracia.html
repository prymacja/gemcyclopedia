Samothracia, a black-colored stone and light [in weight], is similar to burned wood. It takes its name from the island so called. It is found in the mountains between Fano and Pesaro under Monte Castigliano. For under this mountain is a black vein, in which these stones are found. Placed on the fire they emanate the smell of pitch. Its smoke is useful against a mother’s pains [birth pains].
