Exacolitus

Exacolitus

NamesExacolitus. ¹ ²; Excoliceros ³
AppearanceColors of many kinds mixed together. ¹ ² ³
Found inLibya, among the Troglodytes. ³

Exacolitus is a stone of mixed and varied colors. ¹ ² It has a dissolving virtue and, prepared with wine, is used against colic and internal pains.

Generic

Description

Exacolitus is a stone in which many colors are mixed together. ¹ ²

Properties

Exacolitus has a solvent or dissolving virtue, as learned physicians teach. ² Mixed with wine and drunk, it is said to help colic and internal pains. ¹ ² In another preparation, grind the stone, steep it in wine, and drink it to heal colics and iliac passions. ²

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open cited source

Exacolitus is said to be a stone of varied colour. It has a solvent action, according to skilled medical men; and therefore, if mixed with wine and drunk, it is said to be good against colic and internal pains.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Exacolitus is a stone of varied colors all mixed in. It has a dissolving virtue, as learned physicians write. Ground, steeped in wine, and drunk, it heals colics and iliac passions.

3. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 44: Excoliceros

Open cited source

Excoliceros is a little stone, so named to: it is distinguished and diuersed with forti colours, and is full little, and makes men's even that look thereon to quake. And it is found in Libya among the Treglodites.
