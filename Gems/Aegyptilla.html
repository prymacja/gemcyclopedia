Aegyptilla

Aegyptilla

The image shows Nicolo ring stone (source).

NamesAegyptilla; ¹ Egyptilla. ²
AppearanceBlack at the base, blue or cerulean on the surface, with golden veins in one account. ¹ ²
Found inEgypt. ¹

Aegyptilla, also called Egyptilla, is black at the base and blue upon its surface, with some kinds streaked by golden veins. Ground in water, it gives the water the color and taste of wine. ² It is named for Egypt. ¹

Generic

Description

Aegyptilla is named after Egypt, where it is found. ¹ It is black at its base and blue upon its surface; ¹ some kinds have golden veins. ²

Properties
