Poeanites

NamesPoeanites. ¹
AppearanceIts appearance is not described; ¹ it is said to move within its mine like a creature in labor.
Found inThe mountains of Macedonia. ¹

Poeanites is found in the mountains of Macedonia. ¹ Within the mine it heaves with motions like the pangs of childbirth, as though the earth conceived and brought forth a stone. ¹ Pregnant women trust in its protection at the dangerous moment of delivery.

Generic

Description

Poeanites is a stone of the Macedonian mines. ¹ It heaves with motions likened to labor pains, and its generation is compared to the earth conceiving and giving birth.

Properties

Pregnant women trust Poeanites for protection during childbirth, especially at the dangerous moment of delivery. ¹

Sources

1. Marbodus of Rennes — De lapidibus, XXXIV

Open the cited text

The mountains of the Macedonian bold Within their mines the Poeanites hold, Unknown the cause, with imitative throes It heaves, and all the pangs of childbirth knows. From some mysterious seed the wondrous earth Conceives, and in due time excludes the birth; Hence teeming females its protection bless In that last moment when their dangers press.
