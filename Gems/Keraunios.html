Keraunios

Keraunios, also called Keronion or Karchedonios, is described as slightly resembling Ruby. It is called wine-like Keronion, shares Ruby’s reported place of discovery near Karchedon in Libya, and is said to be “not delightful.” The Greek adjective Καρχηδόνιος means “Carthaginian,” which makes a derivation from Καρχηδών (Carthage) plausible; the connection to the gem name remains uncertain. ² No independent description of the stone is known beyond this passage. ¹
