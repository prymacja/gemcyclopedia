Peranites

Peranites

NamesPeranites. ¹; Pionite ²; Pionites ²
AppearanceNo color or shape is specified. ¹ ²
Found inMacedonia. ¹ ²

Peranites is a stone found in Macedonia, said to be of the female sex: in its season it conceives and gives birth to a natural stone like itself. ¹ It is held to benefit pregnant women. ¹

Generic

Description

Peranites is described as a stone of the female sex. ¹ At a certain season it conceives and bears another natural stone like itself.

Properties

Peranites is said to be good for pregnant women. ¹

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open the cited text

Peranites is a stone occurring in Macedonia. It is of the female sex; for at a certain season it is said to conceive and give birth to another natural stone like itself. And it is said to be good for pregnant women.

2. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 79: Pionite

Open cited source

Pionites is a stone that is said to be of female kind, as it is said. At certain time it conceiueth and bears such another stone, and helps women with child as Dioscorides says.
