Ydrinus, also called serpentine by some, draws excessive moisture from the body and has several further medicinal virtues; its use requires caution because it may also remove the body’s natural moisture. ¹

Generic

Ydrinus is called serpentine by some. ¹ Its powers concern rheum, excess bodily moisture, poisonous worms and bladder stones.

Properties

Ydrinus heals rheum and excessive humidity in the human body. ¹ For dropsy, the sufferer stands in the sun with the stone for three hours; ¹ sweating then expels foul water and restores the body to its former health. The stone must be used cautiously: it removes natural internal moisture as well as excess moisture. It drives away poisonous worms and heals their bites. ¹ If ingested, it breaks bladder stones. ¹

Ydrinus is called serpentine by some. It heals rheum and heals the human body of too much humidity; it restores the bodies of dropsy sufferers to their pristine health if they stand in sun with it for three hours; by sweating, they will release a most fetid water. They say it must be used with caution because this stone eliminates not only extraneous humidity, but the natural and internal humidity as well. It drives away poisonous worms and heals their bites. They say that if it is ingested, it breaks the bladder stones.
