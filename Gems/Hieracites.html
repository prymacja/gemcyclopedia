Hieracites

Hieracites

A feather-patterned agate illustrates the varied, plumage-like surface described for Hieracites (source).

NamesHieracites; ² Hieracitis; ¹ Ierarchites. ²
AppearanceLike a hawk’s or kite’s feather, with varied colors; ¹ some are black. ²

Hieracites, also called Hieracitis or Ierarchites, is a variegated stone colored like a hawk’s or kite’s feather. ¹ ² Some forms are black like Gelachides and share its virtue. ²

Generic

Description

Hieracites takes its name from the hawk and bears the colors of a hawk’s plumage. Its coloring may vary; ¹ some are black like Gelachides. ²

Properties

Black Hieracites shares the virtue of Gelachides. ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies XVI, XV.19

Open cited source

Hieracitis has the color of a hawk (cf. ἱέραξ (hierax), “hawk”).

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Hieracites is a stone similar in color to the feather of a kite but varied in color. Some say that it is the same black color as the gelachides, since it has the same virtue.

Ierarchites is the same as Hieracites.
