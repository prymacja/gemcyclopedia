Mithridax

NamesMithridax; ¹ Mithrax; ¹ Mitridax. ²
AppearanceGlitters with various colors in sunlight. ¹ ²
Found inPersia and by the Euphrates. ¹ ²

Mithridax, also called Mithrax or Mitridax, sparkles in many colors when struck by sunlight. ¹ ² It is found in Persia and is also associated with the Euphrates.
