Ophthalmius

NamesOphthalmius. ¹
AppearanceNot described. ¹
Found inNot specified. ¹

Ophthalmius drives every ailment from the eyes and gives its bearer keen sight, while clouding the sight of everyone else. ¹ It is therefore called the guardian of thieves, who may carry away their spoil openly and unharmed.

Generic

Description

No color or place is given for Ophthalmius. Its virtue concerns sight: it heals eye ailments and sharpens the bearer’s vision, but obscures the sight of others. ¹ This makes it a guardian of thieves, allowing them to carry off stolen goods by daylight without harm.

Properties

Ophthalmius chases ailments from the eyes and gives its bearer the sharpest sight. ¹ It clouds all other eyes, so that a thief may carry away plunder openly and securely.

Sources

1. Marbodus of Rennes — De lapidibus, XLIX. Ophthalmius

Open cited stanza

Though from the eyes each ail th' Ophthalmius chase Yet 't is the guardian of the thievish race: It gifts the bearer with acutest sight But clouds all other eyes with thickest night; So that the plunderers bold in open day Secure from harm can bear their spoil away.
