Olea

NamesOlea; ² Olca; ¹ Oica. ¹
AppearanceYellow, black, green, and white. ¹ ²
Found inNot specified. ¹ ²

Olea, also called Olca or Oica, is yellow, black, green, and white. ¹ ² No place of origin or virtue is given.

Generic

Description

Olea is a four-colored stone, yellow, black, green, and white. ² One account calls it Olca or Oica and says it has a barbarian name. ¹

Sources

Open cited source
