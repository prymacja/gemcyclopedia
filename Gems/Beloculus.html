Beloculus, also called Celiculus, is a white stone with a black pupil at its center. ¹ The Syrians used it in sacrificial ornaments dedicated to Belus. ¹ It is said to make its bearer invincible in battle.

Beloculus is a white stone whose center has a black pupil. Because of its beauty, the Syrians used it in all sacrificial ornaments dedicated to the god Belus. It is also said to render its wearer invincible on the battlefield.

Celiculus is the same as Beloculus.
