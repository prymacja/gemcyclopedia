Reyben

Reyben

Reyben is a small, soft, round stone from a crab's head, white or yellow, smooth outside and slightly hollow within. Its preparations are used for bites, teeth, wounds, scabs, and tears. ¹

Generic

Description

Reyben is found in a crab's head. It is white or somewhat yellow, soft, round, smooth on the outside, and slightly hollow within; it is a little harder than the black of a fish's eye. ¹

Properties

Beaten and laid on the wound as a plaster, Reyben cures bites from scorpions and weasels. Taken in a drink, its powder cures bites from rabid dogs. Burned as powder, it cleans the teeth, dries wounds, heals scabs, and prevents tears. ¹

Reyben as Avicenna says, is a little stone and is found in a Crabs head, and is sometimes whife, and is sometimes some deale yellow, and is soft in substance, little harder than the black of a fishes eye, and is in shape round & plain without, and some deale hollow within, and is in virtue kindly cold & moist, and helps against biting of Scorpions, and of the wesell, if it be beaten & laid thereto in plaister wise. Also it is said, it helps against the biting of a mad dogge, if the powder thereof be taken in drink. If the powder thereof be burnt, it cleanses teeth, and dries wounds, and helps scabs, and prevents tears.
