Sardachat is a stone upon which a white, round line appears when it is viewed through. ¹ The passage associates it with Agate, which is called a driver-away of evil and a peace-making stone. ¹

Generic

Description

When viewed through, Sardachat shows a white, round line. ¹ Its entry follows the account of Sardion and Sardonyx and then compares it to Agate.

Properties

The passage links Sardachat with Agate, which myth-makers call a driver-away of evil things; ¹ it therefore gives the name “peace-making” in this context. ¹

Sources

1. St. Epiphanius — On the Twelve Gemstones, The First Stone: Sardion

There is also another, Sardonyx, which is called Molochas; it alleviates the disease Steatomat. But looking through it, the yellowish appears less large than it is, especially during spring growth, when it is the beginning of Easter. There is also another, Sardachat, upon which looking a white round line appears. Like also Agate, about which the myth-makers said that it is a driver away of evil things. Therefore one also calls it peace-making.
