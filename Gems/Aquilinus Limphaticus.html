Generic

Description

The source gives no physical appearance for Aquilinus Limphaticus, but says it is found in some fishes. ¹

Properties

Hung around the neck or carried on the person, Aquilinus Limphaticus wards off the distress of quartan fever. ¹

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Aquilinus Limphaticus, so called for it may be found in some fishes; it is useful to human life. Hung around the neck or carried [on one’s person] it drives away and wards off the misery of quartan fevers.
