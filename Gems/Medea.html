Medea, also called Media, is a black stone with golden or bronze-colored veins. ¹ ² When ground in water, it makes the water saffron-colored and gives it the taste of wine; ¹ it also gives off saffron-colored moisture. ² The stone is linked to the witch Medea, who found it. ¹

Generic

Description

The stone bears the names Medea and Media and is linked to the witch Medea, who found it. It has golden or bronze-colored veins. ¹ When ground in water, it makes the water saffron-colored and gives it the taste of wine; ¹ it is also said to give off saffron-colored moisture. ²

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, Medea

Open cited source

Medea is a stone that takes its name from the witch Medea who found it; it has a black color with golden veins, and when ground in water it tastes like wine and has the color of saffron.

2. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI, XI.4

Open cited source

Media is black, and was discovered by the legendary Medea. It has veins the color of bronze. It gives off saffron-colored moisture and has the flavor of wine.
