Generic

Description

Magnosia is a black stone used in the glassmaking arts. ¹ ² One account identifies it as Alabandicus.

Properties

Magnosia melts and fuses only when the fire is very strong. ¹ ² Once melted, it is mixed with glass to purify the glass’s substance.

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II, Magnesia

Open cited source

Magnesia, which some call magnosia, is a black stone frequently used by glassmakers. This stone melts and fuses if the fire is very strong, but not otherwise; and then, mixed with the glass, it purifies its substance.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, Magnasia or Magnosia

Open cited source

Magnasia or Magnosia is black in color and useful in the glass arts. It is the same as the Alabanticus [Alabandicus].
