Obtalius

NamesObtalius; ¹ Obtalmius. ¹
AppearanceNot given; ¹ some say it may be multicolored. ¹
Found inNot specified. ¹

Obtalius, also called Obtalmius, has no known color, though it may be multicolored. ¹ It protects the eyes from illness. ¹ With a laurel leaf, the proper charms, and correct carrying, it sharpens the bearer’s sight and obscures the sight of those nearby. Some believe it to be the same as Leucoptalmus, but this identity is uncertain.

Generic

Description

The stone’s color is unknown, though some say it may be multicolored. ¹ It may be the same as Leucoptalmus, a four-colored stone resembling a wolf’s eye, but the identification remains uncertain.

Properties

Obtalius preserves the eyes from various illnesses. ¹ When carried with a laurel leaf and the proper charms, it sharpens the wearer’s sight and darkens the sight of those around, until they are unable to see.

Obtalius or Obtalmius is a stone whose color is not given by learned men, although some say it may be multicolored. Its marvelous virtue is that of preserving the eyes from various illnesses. If a laurel leaf is added, and the appropriate charms are pronounced and the stone is appropriately carried, it sharpens the sight of whoever wears it, and obfuscates the sight of those around it, to the point that they are unable to see; this is a marvelous virtue.
