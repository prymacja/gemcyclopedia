Zingutte

NamesZingutte ¹; Zinguttes ¹
AppearanceGlassy in color; no further physical detail is given. ¹
Found inNot specified. ¹

Zingutte is a glass-coloured stone. Worn at the neck, it counters “Victipolam,” staunches blood, and relieves raving and frenzy; held to a burning tree, it quenches the flame. ¹

Zingutte is described only as glass-coloured; no further physical detail is given. ¹

Properties

Worn around the neck, Zingutte counters “Victipolam,” staunches blood, and dispels raving and frenzy. Held to a burning tree, it quenches the flame. ¹

Sources

Open cited source

Zinguttes is a stone with glassy colour, if it be borne about the neck, it helps against Victipolam. And staunches blood, and puts off raving and frenzy. And if it be held to a tree that burns, it quenches the flame thereof as Dioscor. says.
