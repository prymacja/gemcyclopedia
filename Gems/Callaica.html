Generic

Description

Callaica is green but pale and exceedingly cloudy. ¹ It beautifies gold, and its name is connected with Greek κάλλος (kallos), “beauty.” The stone projects from its setting like eyes. Another account calls it large, dull, and not limpid, and says it appears pleasing when set in gold. ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI

Open cited source

Callaica is green in color, but pale and exceedingly cloudy. Nothing more pleasantly beautifies (cf. κάλλος (kallos), “beauty”) gold, whence it is named. It originates in India and Germany in icy cliffs, and it sticks out like eyes.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Caldaicus or Callayca is a big, pale green stone, dull, not limpid, unpleasant to the eye; it appears decent if set in gold. It is found in the cold and icy river’s bank of Media and Germany, and it is visible to the naked eye.
