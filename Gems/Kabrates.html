Kabrates (Kakabres) resembles Rock Crystal, with a dusky or cloudy whiteness. ² It gives eloquence, cheerfulness, honor, and amiability, protects against evil accidents, and heals dropsy. ² A figure of Jupiter engraved on it brings good fortune, favor, and dignity; an eagle gives wealth, victory, and eloquence. A similar stone called Cambnites may be the same kind. ¹ ²

Generic

Description

Kabrates is a crystalline stone with a dusky whiteness, like Rock Crystal. ¹ One account calls its name Kabrates or Kakabres. ² He considers Cambnites a possible identification: that stone is crystal-colored and slightly dark, and when tied to the left arm heals dropsy. ² The identity remains uncertain.

Properties

Kabrates makes its bearer eloquent, cheerful, honorable, and amiable; it protects against evil accidents and heals dropsy. ¹ ² When tied to the left arm, the possibly identical Cambnites also heals dropsy.

Engraving

Figure Engraving and effect Source
Jupiter A figure of Jupiter, commonly seated with a baton and globe, or carved as a man with a ram’s head, when engraved on Kabrates, makes its bearer fortunate and amiable, helps him obtain what he desires—especially from clergy—and raises him to dignity and honor. ²
Eagle An eagle engraved on Kabrates or Rock Crystal makes its bearer rich, victorious, and eloquent. ²

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open cited source

Kabrates is a stone similar to rock crystal. And it is reported to confer eloquence and honour and grace, and to be a remedy for dropsy.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, Kabrates

Open Kabrates

Kabrates or Kakabres is a crystal-colored stone with a dusky whiteness. It has the virtue of rendering man eloquent and cheery. It gives him honors, renders him amiable, protects him from evil accidents, and heals dropsy.

Open possible Cambnites identification

Cambnites is a crystal-colored stone, slightly dark. He who wears it will as a result be gracious to all, affable, and amiable. If tied to the left arm, it heals dropsy. I believe this is the same as Kabrates.

Open Jupiter engraving

The figure of Jupiter is represented as a man, sitting on a chair or a four-footed cathedra, holding in the hand a baton and a globe on the other. I have even found one holding an idol, a Cancer, or a Pisces [fish]; and an eagle at his feet. But magicians represent it differently, for they carve the figure of a man with a Ram’s head, and a taloned heel and a thin, elongated chest. If one finds this carved figure on a stone, especially on a kabrates [gagathes] stone, you should know that it renders its wearer lucky, amiable, and allows him to obtain easily what he wishes, especially from the clergy. This stone also raises men to dignity and honors.

Open eagle engraving

Eagle. This image found on a kabrates or crystal renders its wearer rich, victorious, and eloquent.

3. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 58: Kalbrate

Open cited source

Kalbrates is a passing shining stone, like to Crystal: and men suppose, that it gives fair speech and facunditie, worship and grace, and defends from griefes, and from noyous things and venemous, and cures and heals swelling of the liver, and of the spleen, as Dioscorides says.
