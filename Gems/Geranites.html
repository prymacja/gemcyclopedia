Geranites

NamesGeranites. ¹
AppearanceDark-colored. ¹

Geranites is dark in color and highly praised. Rinsed and held in the mouth, it reveals what others think of its bearer and makes women yield to his wishes. Worn while the naked body is smeared with milk and honey, it keeps the swarms away; ¹ when the stone is removed, they attack and sting.

Generic

Description

Geranites is a dark-colored gem, praised among the foremost stones. ¹

Properties

Rinse Geranites and place it in the mouth to know what others think of its bearer. ¹ It also compels women to comply with the bearer’s wishes.

To test its force, smear the naked body with milk and honey and wear the stone. ¹ The swarms will stay away. Remove the stone, and the insects immediately attack, stinging and sucking the blood.

Sources

1. Marbodus of Rennes — De lapidibus, XXX

Experience old the Geranites' praise,
Though dark of hue, amongst the first doth raise:
For put this in thy mouth — first rinsed, and lo!
What others think of thee thou straight shalt know:
Implanted in it is imperious sway
To make all women to thy wish give way.
To test its force thy naked body smear
With milk and honey, and this jewel wear;
Still shall it keep the greedy swarms at bay,
Nor shall the airy host approach their prey:
Remove the stone, instant the hostile brood
Plunge myriad stings and suck the gushing blood.
