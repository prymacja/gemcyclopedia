Hexacontalite

Hexacontalite

NamesHexacontalite; ² Hexaconta; ³ Execonthalitus; ³ Hexecontalithos. ¹
AppearanceSmall, rounded, and variegated with the colors of sixty precious stones. ¹ ² ³
Found inLibya among the Troglodytes. ¹ ² ³

Hexacontalite, also called Hexaconta, Execonthalitus, or Hexecontalithos, is a small, multicolored stone containing the colors of sixty gems. ¹ ² ³ It is found in Libya among the Troglodytes, and its virtues match the colors it bears. ¹

Generic

Description

Hexacontalite is a small, multicolored stone, its small orb bearing the hues of sixty different gems. ² It is found in Libya among the Troglodytes. ¹ ³ Its Greek name joins ἑξήκοντα (hexēkonta, “sixty”) with λίθος (lithos, “stone”).

Properties

The stone bears as many virtues as the colors and gems contained within it. ³

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies XVI, XII.5

Open cited source

Hexecontalithos is a multicolored stone of a small size, whence it has taken this name for itself, for it is sprinkled with such a variety of spots that the colors of sixty gemstones are contained in its small orb (cf. ἑξήκοντα (hexēkonta), “sixty”; λίθος (lithos), “stone”). It occurs in Libya among the Troglodytes.

2. Marbodus of Rennes — De lapidibus, XXXVIII

Open cited source

True to its name, the Hexacontalite
In one small orb doth sixty gems unite;
With numerous hues for scanty size atones
And singly shews the tints of many stones.
Mid Lybia's deserts parched by burning winds
The Troglodyte this rainbow jewel finds.

3. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Execonthalitus or Hexaconta is a stone that has sixty colors in a small circle and is very often found in Libya. They say that it has as many virtues as the ornaments [colors] of precious stones it contains.
