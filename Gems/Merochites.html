Merochites

A green malachite specimen gives an appearance analogue for Merochites (source).

NamesMerochites ¹
AppearanceStrong green, coarser than Emerald, and soft. ¹
Found inArabia. ¹

Merochites is a soft, strong-green stone, coarser in colour than Emerald, and is found in Arabia. It protects children from harmful events. ¹

Generic

Description

Merochites is green like Emerald, but its green is coarser and resembles the colour of mallows. The stone is soft and is found in Arabia. ¹

Properties

Merochites keeps children safe from harmful and evil events. ¹

Sources

1. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 68: Merochite

Open cited source

Merochites is a green stone like to Smaragdus: but it is more boystous green than Smaragdus, and so it has the name of the colour of mallows, as Isid. says: and is bred in Arabia, and is full soft in substance, and nevertheless it is full profitable, as Dioscorides says. And it keeps and saves children, from harmful and evil happes.
