Generic

Description

Cysteolithos, which One account identifies as Spongius, is a whitish stone approaching citron in color. It is found in sea sponges, though it is not beautiful. ¹

Properties

When drunk with strong wine, it heals those troubled with kidney stones. ¹ Hung around a child’s neck, it protects the child from coughing. ¹

Cysteolithos is a stone of whitish coloring bordering on the citron and found in sea sponges. And though it is not beautiful, it heals those that are troubled with kidney stones, if it is drunk with strong wine. Hung around the neck of children, it protects them from coughing.

Spongius is a stone that is the same as Cysteolithos.
