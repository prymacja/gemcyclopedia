Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open the cited text

Quirinus or Quirus is a conjuring stone found in the nest of the hoopoe. If placed on the chest of a sleeping person, it has the virtue of forcing him to reveal all his misdeeds.

2. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 83: Quirin

Open cited source

Qvirin is a stone that is found in Lapwings neasts, as Dioscorides says. This stone bewrayeth and discovers in sleep counsayle and priuitie: for this stone laid and set under a man's head that sleeps, makes him tell as he thinks sleeping, & multiplies wonderfully fantasies. Therefore Witches love that stone, for they work Witchcraft therewith.
