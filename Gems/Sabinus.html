Sabinus

A dark obsidian specimen illustrates the first-described Sabinus form (source).

NamesSabinus. ¹
AppearanceDark or green. ¹
Found inNot specified. ¹

Sabinus is of two kinds. ¹ The dark kind becomes bright when oil is added; ¹ the green kind is extremely resistant to fire. ¹

Generic

Description

Sabinus has a dark kind and a green kind. ¹

Variants

Form Appearance Distinctive property
Dark Sabinus Dark. ¹ Becomes bright when oil is added. ¹
Green Sabinus Green. ¹ Extremely resistant to fire. ¹

Subtype I: Dark Sabinus

Add oil to the dark Sabinus stone, and it becomes bright. ¹

Subtype II: Green Sabinus

The green Sabinus is extremely resistant to fire. ¹
