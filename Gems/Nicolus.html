Description

Nicolus may be yellow on the surface and black beneath, or entirely black. ¹ Some identify it as a kind of Chalcedony, though the identification is uncertain. Its name is said to come from Greek.

Properties

Worn, Nicolus makes its bearer victorious and well-liked by people. ¹

Open cited source

Nicolus is a stone of two colors, yellow on the surface, black underneath, and sometimes completely black. Some believe that it is a kind of chalcedony. They say that it takes its name from the Greek. Its virtue renders the wearer victorious and well-liked by people.
