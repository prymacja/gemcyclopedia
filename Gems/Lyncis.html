Lyncis is formed from the urine of the lynx, but it is distinct from Ligurius. It remains soft while buried and hardens in a dry place; while still moist underground, it can produce mushrooms. The stone and its mushrooms heal kidney stones, stomach pain, flux of the belly, and jaundice. ¹

Generic

Description

Lyncis forms from the urine of the lynx. ¹ Unlike Ligurius, it stays soft while hidden underground and hardens when placed in a dry place. ¹ If it remains underground or in a humid place before drying, it produces mushrooms. ¹

Properties

The stone heals those troubled by kidney stones, stomach pain, flux of the belly, and jaundice. ¹ Its mushrooms have the same virtue against kidney stones. ¹

Open cited source

Lyncis, too, is a stone generated by the urine of the animal by the same name, but it is different from the abovementioned, because as long as it is hidden underground it is soft, but when it is put in an arid place, it hardens. It is white in color, mixed with black. And if it is kept underground or in a humid place, before it dries up, it will generate funguses [mushrooms]. Its virtue, and the virtue of its funguses [mushrooms], is to heal those troubled by [kidney] stones. It heals stomach pains, halts the flux of the belly, and cures jaundice.
