Ziazaa

FieldAccount
NamesZiazaa. ¹
AppearanceWhite, black, and other colors mingle so that none remains distinct. ¹
Found inIts name derives from its place of origin, which is not specified here. ¹

Ziazaa is a stone of many mingled colors. ¹ It makes its wearer contentious and causes terrible visions in sleep.

Generic

Ziazaa is mixed with white, black, and various other colors, none distinct from the others. ¹

Properties

Ziazaa makes its wearer litigious and causes the wearer to see horrible things in sleep. ¹

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, “Ziazaa”

Open the cited text

Ziazaa takes its name from its place [of origin]. It is mixed with white, black, and various other colors, so that none remain distinct from the other. It renders its wearer litigious and makes him see horrible things in his sleep.
