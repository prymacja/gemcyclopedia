Ostolanus

Ostolanus

NamesOstolanus ¹; Ostaines ¹
AppearanceDeliberately undisclosed. ¹
Found inNot specified. ¹

Ostolanus makes its bearer invisible and clouds the sight of nearby people without impairing the bearer's own vision. Its colour is deliberately withheld. ¹

Generic

Description

Ostolanus, also called Ostaines, is a stone whose colour is not disclosed, lest it be easily found. ¹

Properties

It makes its bearer invisible and draws a cloud over the eyes of nearby people, while the bearer retains clear sight. The stone is called a patron of thieves. ¹

Sources

1. Thomas Cantimpre — Liber de natura rerum, Book XIV: “On Precious Stones”, Ostolanus

Open cited source

Ostolanus, or ostaines, is a stone that is the patron of thieves, because it makes its bearer invisible and draws a cloud over the eyes of those standing nearby, while leaving the bearer's own vision unimpaired. The author does not reveal the colour of this stone, lest it be easily found.
