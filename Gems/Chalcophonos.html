Generic

Description

Chalcophonos is black. ¹ When struck with a stone, it rings like bronze. ¹ ²

Properties

Carried with due reverence, Chalcophonos brings great advantage to the pleader. ² Worn chastely, it gives a melodious voice and protects strained throats from hoarseness. ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies XVI.xv.9

Open cited source

Chalcophonos is black, but when struck with a stone it rings like bronze. — Etymologies XVI.xv.9

2. Marbodus of Rennes — De lapidibus

Open cited source

Like tinkling bronze the Chalcophonos rings
And to the pleader vast advantage brings:
For chastely worn it gives melodious notes
And from rough hoarseness guards their straining throats.
The stone conspicuous for its sable hue
These gifts bestows if borne with reverence due.

3. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 59: Kalcophano

Open cited source

Kalcophanus is a black stone, & makes cleere voice, and defends the grief of hoarcenesse, if it be borne in the mouth, as it is said in Lapidario.

4. Thomas Cantimpre — Liber de natura rerum, Book XIV: “On Precious Stones”, Cacephanus

Open cited source

Cacephanus is a black stone. If carried by a chaste person, it makes the voice sweet and resonant and protects the throat against hoarseness. The stone itself also gives a sweet, ringing sound like bronze when struck with iron or bronze.
