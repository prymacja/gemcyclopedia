AppearanceShaped like a human tongue. ¹ ²

Glossopetra, also called Glosopetra or Gulosus, is shaped like a human tongue and falls from the sky as the moon wanes, for it is not generated by the earth. ² Magicians use it to move the moon. ¹ ²

Generic

Description

Glossopetra takes its name from the Greek words γλῶσσα (glōssa, “tongue”) and πέτρος (petros, “stone”), for it resembles a human tongue. ¹ It is not born from the earth but falls from the sky as the moon wanes. ¹ ²

Properties

Magicians use Glossopetra to move the moon. ¹ ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI

Open cited source

Glossopetra is like the human tongue and took its name for this reason (cf. γλῶσσα (glōssa), “tongue”; πέτρος (petros), “rock”). It is said to drop from the sky when the moon is waning. Magicians attribute no small amount of power to it, for they suppose that by means of it the moon can be made to move.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Glosopetra or Gulosus is a stone similar to a human tongue, from which it takes its name. They say that is not generated by the earth, but that it falls from the sky when the moon wanes. Magicians attribute it a not unsubstantial power in their arts, therefore they say that with it they can incite lunar motions.
