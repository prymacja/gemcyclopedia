Chalazia

Chalazia

The image shows Quartz pebble 2 (source).

NamesChalazia; ¹ ² Chalazias; ¹ ² Chalazius. ²
AppearanceWhite and hail-shaped, with diamond-like hardness. ¹ ²

Chalazia, also called Chalazias or Chalazius, is white and shaped like hail. ¹ ² Its hardness resists blows, and it remains cold even in fire. Its name is linked to Greek χάλαζα (chalaza), “hail.”

Generic

Description

Chalazia is white and shaped like a hailstone. ¹ ² It is extremely hard, like Diamond, and its coldness withstands the fire. ² The name is associated with Greek χάλαζα (chalaza), “hail.”

Properties

No force of blows can tame Chalazia. ¹ Even when placed in fire, it retains its coldness; ² no furnace flame can warm it. ¹

Open cited source: white gems

Chalazias has the shape and whiteness of a hailstone (cf. χάλαζα (chalaza), “hail”); it is also invincibly hard, like a diamond. Even when placed on the fire it retains its coldness.

Open cited source: crystalline gems

There are six kinds of diamond. Chalazias (i.e. chalazius) possesses the whiteness and shape of a hailstone (cf. χάλαζα (chalaza), “hail”) and a diamondlike hardness. Even when placed in the fire it retains its coldness.
