Cyanica, also called Cyaneus, is a Scythian stone of cerulean blue with purple sparkles, golden drops, and sometimes shining spots of varied colors. It has male and female kinds; ¹ the male is clearer and purer, and its golden particles make it more pleasing.

Generic

Description

Cyanica or Cyaneus is found in Scythia and occurs in male and female kinds. ¹ It is cerulean, with purple sparkles, golden drops, and sometimes little shining spots of various colors. ¹ The male kind is more limpid and pure than the female, and resplendent particles of gold make it especially beautiful. It resembles the Cyanea kind of Lapis lazuli in name, color, gold flecks, and Scythian origin, though the source does not expressly identify them as the same stone.

Cyanica or Cyaneus is a cerulean stone with purplish sparkles, varied with golden drops; and sometimes with little shining spots intermixed of different colors. It is found in Scythia and is both male and female. The male is more limpid and pure and pretty than the female, as is rendered pleasant by some resplendent golden particles found in it.
