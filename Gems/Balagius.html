Balagius, also called Balasius, Balas Ruby, Palatius, and Placidus, is a bright, transparent red gem, sometimes purplish or rosy, sparkling and fiery. It is called the weaker female of Carbuncle or its “house”; ¹ ² one stone was seen with a Balagius exterior and Carbuncle within.

Generic

Description

Balagius is bright, transparent, red, and may be purplish or rosy. ¹ It sparkles and is called Placidus, or “pleasant.” Some regard it as a Carbuncle of lesser color and virtue, or as the Carbuncle’s matrix; ² one stone was seen with a Balagius exterior and Carbuncle interior. ¹

Properties

Balagius has the powers of Carbuncle in a weaker degree. ¹ It overcomes and represses troublesome and lustful thoughts, reconciles quarrels among friends, and brings good health. ² Ground and drunk with water, it heals illnesses of the eyes and aids disorders of the liver. ² Touching the four corners of a house, garden, or vineyard with it is said to protect them from lightning, storms, and worms. ²

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open cited source

Balagius, which is also called palatius, is a gem of a red colour, of very bright material and very transparent substance. It is said to be the female of the carbuncle, for its colour and powers are like those of the carbuncle, but weaker, just as the female is as compared to the male. And some say it is the 'house' of the carbuncle, and therefore it is called its 'palace' (palatium). For carbuncle is frequently produced there: recently, in our own time, one has been seen that was balagius outside and carbuncle inside, in the same stone. Therefore Aristotle says that this stone is a kind of carbuncle.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Balasius (Balas Ruby) is a purplish or rosy-colored stone that sparkles and flames and that some call placidus [pleasant]. Some think it is a carbuncle of lesser color and virtue, just as the virtue of the female carbuncles is different from the males. It has been seen that the external part of this stone is like that of the balasius, and the inside that of the carbuncle, so much so that some say that balassus is the carbuncle’s matrix. Balasius’s virtue overcomes and represses bothersome and luxurious thoughts, reconciles quarrels among friends, and brings good health to the human body. If ground and drunk with water, it heals illnesses of the eyes and aids in liver disorders. And what I believe is even more marvelous is that if you should touch with a balasius the four corners of a house, garden, or vineyard, these will never be hit by lightning or storms, or be infested by worms.
