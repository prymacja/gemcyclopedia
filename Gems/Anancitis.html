Anancitis

Anancitis

NamesAnancitis; ¹ Anancithidus. ²
AppearanceNo physical appearance is described. ¹ ²

Anancitis, also called Anancithidus, is a necromantic stone. ² It calls forth demons and spirits, and during hydromancy it calls out their images. ¹ ² Its physical appearance and place of origin are not described.

Generic

Description

Anancitis is named for its necromantic virtue; ¹ ² no physical form or place of origin is given.

Properties

During hydromancy, Anancitis calls out the images of demons. ¹ It also calls forth demons and spirits. ²
