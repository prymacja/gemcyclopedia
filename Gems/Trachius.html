Trachius

The image shows Onyx (source).

NamesTrachius; ² Thracius. ¹
AppearanceOne kind is black and resonant; ¹ ² another is almost green and opaque. ²
Found inThe river of Equitia called Pontus. ¹

Trachius, spelled Thracius in another account, is black and resonant. another account also records an almost green kind that is not transparent. ² The black kind is found in the river of Equitia called Pontus. ¹

Generic

Description

The black kind of Trachius is resonant. ¹ ² A second kind is almost green, though not transparent. ²

Variants

Kind Appearance Place
Black Trachius Black and resonant. River of Equitia called Pontus. ¹ ²
Green Trachius Almost green, but not transparent. Not specified. ²

Subtype I: Black Trachius

This black kind is resonant and is found in the river of Equitia called Pontus. ¹ ²

Subtype II: Green Trachius

This kind is almost green, but not transparent. ² No place is specified for it.

Sources

Trachius is a stone of two types: one resounding black, and the other almost green, but not transparent.
