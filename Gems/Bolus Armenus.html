NamesBolus Armenus; ¹ ² Ramai. ¹; Rabri ³; Bolus Armenicus ³
AppearanceReddish, sometimes tending toward citrine-yellow with a dusky green tone; ¹ cold and dry. ² ³
Found inA vein of earth in Armenia. ² ³

Bolus Armenus, also called Ramai, is a reddish earth associated with Armenia. ¹ ² Though it is counted among stones for its virtues, it is not itself a stone. ² It is cold and dry, and is used against bowel fluxes, fever, dysentery, menstrual bleeding, bloody expectoration, and disorders of the spleen and stomach.

Generic

Description

Bolus Armenus is a vein of earth found in Armenia. ² Though it is not a stone, it is counted among stones for its virtue. ² Its color is reddish, bordering on citrine yellow, with a dusky green tone; ² its complexion is cold and dry. ² Many counterfeits exist, and only a few are true and good. ²

Properties

Bolus Armenus overcomes looseness of the bowels, especially bleeding associated with dysentery and menstruation. ¹ It is also used against fever and fluxes of the belly, and helps bloody expectoration, splenic ailments, and other disorders of the stomach. ²

It constrains and hardens the womb and staunches blood from any part of the body. ³

Bolus Armenus is also called Rabri. ³ The same name is also used for Ranius, a heavy stone of livid hue tending toward transparent white; these marks distinguish it from the red Armenian earth. ² The shared name therefore belongs to two distinct descriptions.

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open cited source

Ramai, which is mentioned in medical and alchemical books, is the same as Bolus armenus. It is a reddish stone. Experience gives certain proof that it overcomes looseness of the bowels, and especially the bleeding of dysentery and menstruation.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Bolus Armenus is a vein of earth found in Armenia, and even though it is not a stone, it is counted among the stones because of its virtue. Its color is reddish bordering on the citrine, with a dusky green tone. Its complexion is cold and dry. All doctors attribute to it great virtues. It is an efficacious remedy against fever and fluxes of the belly; it helps emoptoics, the splenetics, and other similar disorders of the stomach. There are many counterfeits and only few [bolus armenus remedies] that are true and good, and I have never seen a good one.

3. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 85: Rabri

Open cited source

Rabri is a stone, & has another common name, and is called Bolus Armenicus, and is a stone or a veyne of earth, & is of cold and dry kind, & has a red colour, and is found in Armenia, & has virtue to constrain & make hard the womb, and to staunch blood out of what place of the body it runs.
