Jewstone (Judaicus lapis) is white, about the size of an acorn, and marked with signs like letters, called grammata in Greek. ¹ It is frequently found in Judaea. ¹

Generic

Description

Jewstone is a small white stone, about the size of an acorn, with marks resembling letters. These signs are called grammata in Greek. The stone is frequently found in Judaea. ¹

Judaicus lapis (Jewstone) Isidore likewise says of the Jew stone that it is white and about the size of an acorn, and inscribed with marks like letters, which the Greeks call grammata. Avicenna says it is called Jewstone because it is frequently found in Judaea.
