Dactylus of Ida

Dactylus of Ida

A belemnite fossil embedded in rock, a possible visual analogue for the iron-colored finger-stone (source).

NamesDactylus of Ida; ¹ Idaeus. ²
AppearanceIron-colored and shaped like a human thumb. ¹ ²
Found inCrete, at Mount Ida. ¹ ²

The Dactylus of Ida, also called Idaeus, is an iron-colored stone from Crete, shaped like a human thumb. ¹ ²

Generic

Description

This iron-colored stone is named for its likeness to a human thumb. ¹ It comes from Crete, at Mount Ida. ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies XVI, XV.12

Open cited source

The ‘dactylus of Ida’ is from the island of Crete. It is iron-colored. The reason for its name is because it resembles a human thumb (cf. δάκτυλος (daktylos), “finger”).

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Idaeus is an iron-colored stone. It is found in Mount Ida in Crete, from which it takes its name, and it is shaped like a man’s thumb.
