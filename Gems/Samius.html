Samius is a white, heavy, fragile stone from Samos, used to polish gold. ² Taken in drink, it cures dizziness, calms the mind, and prevents miscarriage. Ground with milk and laid over the eyes, it treats eye ailments; carried in the left hand, it stops long weeping. Yet if bound to a woman’s hand during childbirth, it hinders the birth and keeps the child in the womb. ¹

Generic

Description

Samius is a white, heavy, fragile stone from Samos. ² Craftsmen polish gold with it. ¹ ²

Properties

Taken in drink, Samius cures dizziness, calms the mind, and prevents miscarriage. ¹ If carried in the left hand, it stops tears that have flowed for a long time. ² For other eye ailments, grind the stone with milk and lay it over the eyes.

If Samius is bound to the hand of a woman in childbirth, it hinders the birth and keeps the child in the womb. ¹

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open the cited text

Samius is a stone named from the island of Samos, where it is found. Gold is polished with this stone. It is also said that, taken in drink, it cures dizziness and settles the mind; but it is said to have this disadvantage, that if it is bound to the hand of a woman in childbirth, it hinders the birth and keeps it back in the womb.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open the cited text

Samius is a stone so called after an island that bears the same name, Samos, where it was first found. Craftsmen use it to polish gold. It is white, heavy, and fragile. Its virtue is to take vertigoes away and to allay insanity. If drunk, it prevents miscarriages. If carried in the left hand, it is useful to halt tears shed for a long time, and it is also useful in other illnesses of the eyes if it is first ground with milk and then placed over the eyes.

3. Thomas Cantimpre — Liber de natura rerum, Book XIV: “On Precious Stones”, Samius — Samian Stone

Open cited source

The Samian stone is named after the island of Samos. It is heavy and white. Gold is polished with this stone. When carried, it calms dizziness and restores disturbed minds. It has this harmful property: when tied to the breast of a woman in labour, it is said to hold back childbirth.
