Chrysocolla, also called Crisocollus or Antitaneus, resembles gold and has the nature of a Magnet. ¹ ² It is associated with regions where ants are said to extract gold; ² one account says it increases or coagulates gold, another that it increases wealth. ²

Generic

Description

Chrysocolla resembles gold and has the nature of a Magnet. ¹ ² It is found in places where ants dig out gold: one account names India, another Media. ¹ The name is explained by its power to increase or coagulate gold. ¹

Properties

Chrysocolla has the nature of a Magnet. ¹ One account says it increases or coagulates gold; another says that it increases wealth. ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies XVI.xv.7

Open cited source

Chrysocolla occurs in India, where ants dig out gold. It is similar to gold and has the nature of a magnet, except that it is also reported to increase (i.e. coagulate) gold, whence also it is named. — Etymologies XVI.xv.7

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Crisocollus [Chrysocolla] is a stone similar to gold. It is produced in Media, where ants [formicae] extract gold. It has the property of a magnet and increases wealth.

Antitaneus is the same as Crisocollus.
