Solis Gemma

Solis Gemma

The image shows Beryl-284737.jpg (source).

NamesSolis; ¹ ² Solis Gemma; ² Jewel of the Sun. ²
AppearanceWhite, like Beryl; ² it shines out rays when placed in full sun. ¹ ² ³
Found inNot specified. ¹ ² ³

Solis Gemma, the Jewel of the Sun, is a white stone like Beryl. ² When placed in full sunlight, it sends shining rays all around. It is also held to act against deadly poison that has been drunk. ² The same white, sun-radiating gem is also called Solis. ¹ ²

Generic

Description

Solis Gemma is a white stone likened to Beryl. ² When placed under the full rays of the sun, it emits resplendent beams in every direction. ² The shorter name Solis is given to the white gem that casts its rays in the form of the sun. ¹

Properties

Solis Gemma is held to counter deadly poisons that have been drunk. ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI, X.6, “Solis”

Open the cited text

Solis is a white gem, and has this name because it casts its rays in the form of the sun (sol) shining on the world.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, “Solis Gemma (Jewel of the Sun)”

Open the cited text

Solis Gemma [Jewel of the Sun] is white in color, similar to a beryl. And placed under the sun’s rays, under the full blaze of that celestial body, it emits resplendent beams all around and from this it takes its name. It is a stone of great virtue against deadly poisons that have been drunk.

3. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 91: Solis gemma

Open cited source

The Sun stone is called solis gemma, and is white shining, and has that name for it shines with beams, as the Sun does shine in the world, as Isidore says De candidis gemmis.
