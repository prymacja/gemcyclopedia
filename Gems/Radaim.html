Radaim

Radaim

Black obsidian shown as a visual analogue for Radaim’s shining black appearance (source).

NamesRadaim; ¹ ² Donatides; ¹ Doriatides. ²
AppearanceShining black, and black and transparent. ¹ ²
Found inThe head of a cock or rooster, a catfish, or a beast called a murilage; ¹ ² some sources mention fowls generally. ¹

Radaim, Donatides, and Doriatides are names for a shining black stone found in the head of a bird or other beast. ¹ ² It is recovered after ants have eaten the severed head or the animal’s flesh. ² The stone grants wishes, brings honor, and aids in governing. ²

Generic

Description

Radaim is a shining black stone found in an animal’s head. ¹ ² Its color is described as both bright black and transparent. ² It may be found in the head of a rooster or cock; ¹ other accounts name a catfish, a murilage, or fowls more generally.

One method gives the severed heads of fowls to ants; after a long time, the stone may be found in a cock’s head. ¹ Another cuts off the beast’s head and lets ants eat its flesh, after which the stone can be retrieved. ²

Properties

Radaim enables its possessor to obtain whatever he wishes. ¹ ² It heals its wearer, brings him honor, and aids him in governing. ²

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open the cited text

Radaim and donatides are said to be the same stone. And they say that it is shining black. It is reported that, when the heads of fowls are given to ants to eat, after a long time this stone is sometimes found in the head of a cock. And it is said to enable one to obtain whatever he wishes.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open the Radaim passage · Open the Doriatides passage

Radaim is a black and transparent stone. It is found in the rooster’s head, even though some say in the head of a catfish, as mentioned above in the Doriatides entry. When cut and immediately placed somewhere so that ants may eat its flesh, once the latter is eaten, the stone can be retrieved. It brings honor to whoever wears it, and it is useful in governing.

Doriatides is a stone found in the head of an animal called murilage, when the head has been suddenly cut off, and it has been given to the ants to eat it; its color becomes bright black. Some say that it can be extracted from a rooster’s head and more may be found under the Radaim heading. Its virtue ensures that a man may have his every wish satisfied.
