Generic

Description

Iacinthizonta is a crystal from India, similar to Hyacinth. ¹ Its crossed, discolored veins are its distinguishing mark; its name is explained through the Greek word ζώνη (zōnē), “stripe.”

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies XVI, IX.4

Open cited source

  1. Iacinthizonta is from India, and closely resembles the hyacinth-stone. Certain crystals of these, similar to iacinthus, are discolored by crisscrossing veins, and from this flaw comes their name (cf. ζώνη (zōnē), “stripe”).
