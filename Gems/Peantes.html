Peantes

Peantes

A pale blue fluorite crystal illustrates the icy-water colour (source).

NamesPeantes; ¹ Peantides. ¹
AppearanceThe color of water turned to ice by cold. ¹
Found inMacedonia. ¹

Peantes, also called Peantides, is an icy-colored stone found in Macedonia. ¹ It is spoken of as female: an older account says that it conceives at certain times and gives birth to a like stone. ¹ Another reading rejects this as literal, taking the stone’s feminine nature to mean that its virtues aid women in conceiving and giving birth; the matter is left open to judgment.

Generic

Description

Peantes is a stone of the female sex, also named Peantides. ¹ Its color resembles water frozen by cold, and it is found in Macedonia. ¹

Properties

An older account says that the female stone can conceive at certain times and bear a stone like itself. ¹ Another interpretation says that “female” refers not to the stone’s own conception, but to its virtues helping women to conceive and give birth. ¹ The correctness of this interpretation is left for others to judge.

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, Peantes or Peantides

Open cited passage

Peantes or Peantides (as some say) is a stone of the female sex, as it is able to conceive at certain times, and it gives birth to a stone similar to herself. Even though some have written thus, I do not like it; rather, I believe that such writers are in error because they misunderstood the words of the ancients. For when they say that this stone is of the female sex, they mean not that it conceives, but rather that its virtues lend help to women in conceiving and in giving birth. Whether this opinion is correct, I leave others to judge. It is found in Macedonia. The color of this stone is like water turned to ice by the cold.
