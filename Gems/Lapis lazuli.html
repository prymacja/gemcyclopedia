Lapis lazuli, also called Zemech, Zumemellazuli, and Lazolus, is a blue stone gleaming with gold flecks. ³ Cyanea is a Scythian kind, either pure blue or flecked with gold. ² Lapis lazuli yields azure pigment and is a remedy for melancholic illnesses. ¹ ³

Generic

Description

Lapis lazuli has the color of a serene sky, though it is not transparent. ³ It gleams with gold flecks like stars in the night sky and endures fire. ³ Some call it the celestial or starry stone. ³ Cyanea is a kind of lapis lazuli from Scythia; ² it shines blue, either purely or with flickering gold flecks.

Azure pigment is made from lapis lazuli; ¹ this is the ultramarine azure color. ³

Properties

Prepared as physicians direct, lapis lazuli cures melancholic illnesses. ³ It is also a sure remedy for black bile, quartan fever, and fainting caused by black bile vapors. ¹

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open cited source

Zemech is the stone [also] called lapis lazuli; in it there is a pale blue colour with small golden specks. The pigment azure (azurium) is made from it. It is taken as a sure remedy for black bile and quartan fever, and for fainting caused by the vapours of black bile.

2. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI, IX.7

Open cited source

Cyanea (i.e. a type of lapis lazuli) is a gem from Scythia glittering with a blue sheen, either pure blue, or sometimes varied with flecks of flickering gold.

3. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Zumemellazuli or Zemech is called lapis lazuli in Latin. It has a yellow hue contained within the color of the sky when it is at its most serene, not transparent; it glitters with gold flecks like the night sky and endures [being exposed to] fire. Because of its beauty, it is called the celestial or starry stone. Prepared as doctors instruct, it cures melancholic illnesses. From this is made that color called ultramarine azure.

Lazolus is a stone described under the heading of Zumemelazoli [Zumemellazuli].

4. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 103: Zimiech

Open cited source

Zimiech is a stone or vein of the earth, whereof Lapis Lazurij is made; as it is said in Lapidary. The more liker this stone is to the colour of heaven, the better it is, and has small pieces, as it were of gold mingled thereon. Those that be whitest be most earthy, and therefore they be not so precious. These may be long kept without corruption: and help against many evils, and against swooning, that comes of melancholic fumositie, if they be given in due manner to the patient. It shall never be given without it be ground full final, & oft washed full clean. And the sign of perfect washing thereof is, if the water that it is washed in, be little or naught died. This stone cleanses & looses wonderfully the quarteine, and shall not be given with the decostion. For then it should fall to the bottom, but after or before, & that, with whey, as Dioscorides says, as it is said in Plat. And as Cerusa is made of plates of iron: so is Lazurum made of plates of silver with vinegar. Oft the stone Lazurius is found among silver ore, & among gold ore. And oft in the veins of Lazarius be found Sapphires, Iacincts, and other such blue stones.
