Limacie is a white stone, slightly translucent and about the size of a fingernail clipping. ¹ It is extracted from the head of a shell-less slug, which lives in damp, rocky places; ¹ squeeze the slug’s head as soon as it is sighted to obtain the stone. ¹ Hung around the neck, Limacie frees its bearer from fever. ¹

Generic

Description

Limacie is found in the head of a shell-less slug. ¹ To extract it, squeeze the slug’s head as soon as the animal is sighted. ¹ The slug inhabits damp, rocky places. The stone is white, slightly translucent, and small as a fingernail clipping.

Properties

Hung around the neck, Limacie frees its bearer from fever. ¹

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum, Limacie

Open cited source

Limacie is stone that takes its name from the animal in whose head it is found. Thus it is extracted from the head of slugs without shell, whose natural habitat is damp and rocky. It must be extracted by squeezing its head as soon as the slug is sighted. It is of a white color with little transparency, and it is small in size and similar to a fingernail cutting. They say that hung by the neck it frees man from fever.
