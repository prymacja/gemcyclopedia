Cimedia is a stone found in the head and along the backbone of a fish of the same name. ¹ It is round and seven fingers long, with a large, transparent head through which its spine can be seen when held to the light. ¹

Generic

Description

Cimedia is taken from a fish bearing the same name. Two kinds are found in the fish’s head; ¹ a third lies near the third vertebra, beside the backbone joint and toward the tail. Kimedini Limphatici is another name for Cimedia. ¹

Properties

Cimedia foretells calm or stormy weather in the air and at sea. Taken with water, it makes a man lusty during the day. ¹

Cimedia is a stone extracted from the brain of the fish by the same name. Two kinds may be found in the head; a third may be found near the third vertebra, near the joint of the backbone, toward the fish’s tail. It is round and seven fingers long; its head is large and transparent so that when it is placed against the light the spine becomes visible. Magicians say that its virtue is that of foretelling calmness or storminess of air and sea. If drunk with water, it renders man lusty during the day.

Kimedini Limphatici is the same as Cimedia.
