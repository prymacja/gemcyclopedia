Pontica

Pontica

NamesPontica. ¹ ² ³
AppearanceTransparent cerulean or sea-blue; ³ marked with red or golden drops and stars, or streaked with long red lines. ¹ ² ³
Found inThe Pontus, or Black Sea. ¹ ³

Pontica is a transparent, sea-blue gem from the Pontus, or Black Sea. ¹ ² ³ It bears red drops or stars, golden marks, or long red lines. ³ By this stone a person may speak with demons, drive them away, and compel them to answer. ²

Generic

Description

Pontica is a transparent blue stone resembling the waters of the Pontus. ² Its three forms are distinguished by their markings: one has red stars or blood-colored drops, another golden marks, and the third long red lines mixed with the blue. ¹ ³

Properties

By Pontica one may question demons and drive them away. ² They are compelled to answer the one who questions them. ³

Variants

Form Appearance
Red-starred Pontica Sea-blue, sprinkled with red stars or blood-colored drops. ¹ ³
Golden-marked Pontica Sea-blue, marked with gold or golden stars. ¹ ² ³
Red-lined Pontica Long red lines mixed with its cerulean color. ¹ ³

Subtype I: Red-Starred Pontica

This transparent, sea-blue kind is sprinkled with red stars or blood-colored drops. ¹ ³

Subtype II: Golden-Marked Pontica

This transparent, sea-blue kind shines with gold or golden stars. ¹ ² ³

Subtype III: Red-Lined Pontica

This transparent, cerulean kind is streaked with long red lines. ¹ ³

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI, IV

Open the cited text

Pontica stones are named after the Pontus (i.e. the Black Sea), and come in different varieties, at one time glittering with blood red spots, at another with gold spots. Some have stars and some are streaked with long lines of color.

2. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI, XXVI

Open the cited text

Pontica is a certain gemstone that is transparently blue. It has reddish stars, and sometimes golden ones as well. People say that they can question demons and drive them away by means of this stone.

3. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open the cited text

Pontica is a transparent cerulean gem. I find that there are three well-known species. It takes its names from the Pontus Sea, where it is found, and because it is similar to its seawater. [In one species,] the sea blue color is sprinkled with red stars or blood-colored drops. The other shines with golden marks. The third is streaked with long red lines mixed with its cerulean color. They say that thanks to this stone one may speak with the devil as well as chase him away and is compelled to answer whoever questions him.
