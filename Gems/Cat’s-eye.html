NamesCat’s-eye; ¹ Beli oculus. ¹
AppearanceWhite around a black pupil, with a golden gleam shining from within. ¹

Cat’s-eye, called beli oculus, is a white gem surrounding a black pupil that shines with a golden gleam from within. ¹ Because of its beauty it was dedicated to Belus, king of Assyria. ¹

Generic

Description

Cat’s-eye is white around a black pupil, lit at its center by a golden gleam. ¹ Its beauty led to its dedication to Belus, king of Assyria, and gives rise to the name beli oculus. ¹

Open cited source

Cat’s-eye (beli oculus) is white surrounding a black pupil lit from the middle with a golden gleam. Because of its beauty it was dedicated to Belus, king of Assyria, and thus it is named. — Etymologies XVI.x.9
