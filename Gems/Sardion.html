Sardion

The image shows Carnelian sard (source).

NamesSardion. ¹
AppearanceFiery and blood-like, transparent, and resembling a pickled, cut-open fish. ¹
Found inBabylon near Assyria. ¹

Sardion is a transparent, fiery, blood-red stone found in Babylon near Assyria. ¹ Its cut, fish-like appearance gives it its name. ¹ Applied by anointing, it heals swellings and ulcers, and it is also used for wounds made by the sword. ¹ It is a separate entry from Sard in the source tradition.

Generic

Description

Sardion is transparent and blood-red, with a fiery appearance. ¹ Its resemblance to a pickled, cut-open fish is given as the reason for its name.

Properties

Sardion heals swellings and ulcers when used to anoint them. ¹ It is also used against other wounds cut by the sword. ¹

Sardion, the Babylonian: so named because it is fiery and blood-like in appearance. Sardion: it resembles a pickled, cut-open fish, which is why it was also called Sardion. It received its naming from its appearance. However, it is found in Babylon near Assyria. The stone is also transparent. In terms of power it is healing, because it is used for healing against swellings and ulcers when one anoints them. Also for other wounds that are cut by the sword.
