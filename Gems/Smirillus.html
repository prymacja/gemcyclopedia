Smirillus

Smirillus

NamesSmirillus; ¹ Smeriglius; ¹ Hysmeri. ¹
AppearanceIron-colored and very hard. ¹
Found inMany places. ¹
UseCuts and planes stones and polishes weapons. ¹

Smirillus, also called Smeriglius or Hysmeri, is an iron-colored, very hard stone that abrades every material except diamond. ¹ It cuts and planes other stones and polishes weapons. ¹

Generic

Description

Smirillus is a very hard, iron-colored stone. ¹ Like a file or saw, it wears away every material except diamond. It is used to cut and plane stones and to polish weapons. ¹

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited passage: Hysmeri and Smeriglius

Hysmeri is the same as Smeriglius [Smirillus].

Open cited passage: Smirillus

Smirillus is the file and serpent of all things, except of the diamond; it consumes and corrodes all things. It is a stone of an iron color and is very hard; it is found in many various localities. It is used to cut and plane stones and to clean [polish] weapons.
