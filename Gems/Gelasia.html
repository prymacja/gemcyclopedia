Gelasia

Gelasia

A white quartz specimen approximates Gelasia’s hail-white appearance (source).

NamesGelasia ²; Gelacia ¹
AppearanceWhite like a hailstone and very hard. ¹ ²
Found inNot specified. ¹ ²

Gelasia is white like hail and hard as diamond. It cannot be heated by fire and remains cold. ¹ ²

Generic

Description

Gelasia is a white precious stone shaped like a hailstone. ¹ It has the whiteness of hail and hardness like diamond. ²

Properties

It never warms in fire and always remains cold. ¹ ²

Sources

1. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 51: Gelacia

Open cited source

Gelacia is a white precious stone, shaped as an haile stone: & it is so cold that it never heats with fire, as Isidore says, and Dioscorides also.

2. Thomas Cantimpre — Liber de natura rerum, Book XIV: “On Precious Stones”, Gelasia

Open cited source

Gelasia is a stone possessing the whiteness of hail and an unconquerable hardness like that of diamond. It can never be heated by fire, but always remains cold.
