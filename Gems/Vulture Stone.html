Vulture Stone

Vulture Stone

NamesVulturis Lapis; ¹ Quaidros; ¹ Quandros; ² Vulture Stone. ²
AppearanceNot described. ¹ ² ³
Found inThe brain of the vulture. ² One account says it is found there when the bird’s head is suddenly severed; ¹ another says it is sometimes found there. ¹ ² ³

Vulturis Lapis, also called Quaidros and Quandros, is a stone found in the brain of a vulture. ¹ ² It heals its wearer, protects against misfortune, fills a woman’s breasts with milk, and brings success to those who ask it. ¹

Generic

Description

Vulturis Lapis is found in a vulture’s brain. ¹ ² It is sometimes present there; another account says it is recovered by suddenly severing the bird’s head.

Properties

The stone heals the one who wears it, protects against every kind of misfortune, fills a woman’s breasts with milk, and ensures success for those who ask for it. ¹ ²

Sources

1. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open the Vulturis Lapis passage · Open the Quaidros alias

Vulturis Lapis is so called from the bird by the same name; by suddenly cutting its head off, the stone is found in its brain. It heals those who wear it. It fills a woman’s breast with milk. It ensures success to those who ask for it.

Quaidros is the same as the Vulturis Lapis, as we will say later on.

2. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open the cited text

Quandros is a stone sometimes found in the brain of a vulture. Its power is said to be good against any kind of misfortune; and it fills the breasts with milk.

3. Bartholomaeus Anglicanus — De proprietatibus rerum, Book XVI, Chapter 84: Quandros

Open cited source

Qvandros is a stone of vile colour, but it is of great virtue, as Dios, says, and is found in the head of a vulture: and helps against all evil causes, and fills teats full of milke.
