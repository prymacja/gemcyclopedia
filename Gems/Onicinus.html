Onicinus is a gum from a tree of the same name, hardened like Amber and therefore counted among stones. It is white with some red. ¹ Placed over coals, it gives off a sweet smell, whitens the face, and heals scabies.

Description

Onicinus is a gum from a tree of the same name. ¹ It hardens like Amber, so it is included among stones. It is white mixed with some red. ¹

Properties

When placed over coals, Onicinus gives off a sweet smell, whitens the face, and heals scabies. ¹

Open cited source

Onicinus even though it is a gum, from the tree with the same name, it is listed among the stones for it hardens in the same manner, as it will be said of succinus [amber]. It is white mixed with some red. If placed over coals it emanates a sweet smell, whitens one’s countenance, and heals scabies.
