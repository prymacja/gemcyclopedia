Pale grey pumice (source).

NamesTephrias. ¹
AppearanceThe color of ashes. ¹
Found inNot specified. ¹

Generic

Description

Tephrias takes its name from its ash-like color. ¹

Properties

When worn bound on the body, Tephrias protects against serpents. ¹

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies, Book XVI, V.18, “Tephrias”

Open the cited text

Tephrias is named from the color of ashes (cf. τέφρα (tephra), “ashes”). This stone is praised as a protection against serpents when it is worn, bound on [the body].
