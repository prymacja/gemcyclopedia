Tecollites

NamesTecollites. ¹
AppearancePlain and olive-pit-like. ¹
Found inNot specified. ¹

Tecollites is an unassuming stone like an olive pit. ¹ When powdered and drunk in water, it dissolves urinary stones. ¹

Generic

Description

Tecollites is humble in appearance and resembles an olive pit. ¹

Properties

Powder Tecollites and drink it in water to dissolve urinary stones. ¹

Sources

1. Marbodus — De lapidibus, LV. Tecollites

Open the cited text

Of humble aspect, but of virtue rare. / Like olive stones the Tecollites appear: / Powdered, in water by the patient quaffed, / The torturing stone dissolves the potent draught.
