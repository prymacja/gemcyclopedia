Evanthus

Evanthus

A colorful leopard-skin jasper cabochon shown as a visual analogue for Evanthus’s variegated, panther-like markings (source).

NamesEvanthus; ¹ Panthera. ¹
AppearanceOne body bears mixed colors and markings of black, red, pale, green, rosy, and purple; ¹ its shape resembles the animal called panther.
Found inMedia. ¹

Evanthus, also called Panthera, is a many-colored stone found in Media. ¹ Its markings may be black, red, pale, green, rosy, or purple. ¹ Looked upon as the sun rises, it brings victory in every undertaking that day; its further virtues vary with the stones whose colors are mingled within it.

Generic

Description

Evanthus bears many colors mixed together in one body, with a shape like the panther. Black, red, pale, green, rosy, and purple markings may appear upon it. ¹ It is found in Media. ¹

Properties

Whoever looks upon Evanthus as the sun rises will be victorious in all undertakings of that day. ¹ The stone also bears diverse virtues according to the varieties of other stones mingled within it, for each stone lends it its own virtue.

Open cited source

Panthera is also called evanthus; it is a stone that has various colors mixed in one body whose shape is similar to the animal by the same name, so called because of the variety of its colors. Just like it, this stone may have black, red, pale, green, rosy, and purple marks. It is found in Media. If anyone looks at this stone as the sun rises, he will be victorious on all actions of that day. It is also said that the same stone has various virtues, as many as the mixtures of other stones in it, because each stone lends it its own virtue.
