Celontes, also called Celonites and Drome, has three kinds: uterine, cephalic, and hepatic. ² The accounts differ over whether it is found within shellfish or within a tortoise. ¹

Generic

Description

Celontes is purple and is found in the body of a shellfish, according to one account. ¹ Another describes three kinds taken from a large tortoise whose shell is the color of Pearl: the uterine kind, which may be purple or variegated; the cephalic kind, taken from the head; ² and the hepatic kind, taken from the liver. ²

Properties

Held under the tongue, Celontes foretells future events, but its virtue is bound to the moon. One account gives the first day of the lunar month while the moon is rising and waxing, and the twenty-ninth day while it is waning. ¹ Another gives the first day of a new moon and the fifteenth day after the moon’s waxing, from sunrise until the sixth hour; ² during the waning phase, its virtue acts before dawn. Before placing it under the tongue, wash the mouth. Celontes is not harmed by fire. These stones are also called Drome; ² carried with peony roots, they make the bearer very powerful.

Sources

1. St. Albertus Magnus — The Book of Minerals, Tractate II

Open cited source

Celontes is a stone of a purple colour, and it is said to be found in the body of a shellfish; for some very large shellfish have dwellings that gleam with a pearly lustre. It is said that if one holds it under the tongue it makes him able to divine the future. But it is said to have this power only on the first day of the lunar month, when the moon is rising and waxing, and again on the twenty-ninth day when the moon is waning. Also it is said that this stone is not injured by fire.

2. Camillo Leonardi — Speculum Lapidum

Open cited source

Celonites or Celontes is a stone of three types, as some say. It is extracted from a large tortoise whose shell is the color of a pearl. There is another type called uterine, which may be purplish or of varied color, and its property is that of not being destroyed by fire. Because of this virtue the stone deserves particular praise: at an appropriate time, having washed one’s mouth, whoever shall place it under the tongue, it is said that immediately that person will foretell things to come as if he had received a divine spirit. The appropriate time for this is the whole day of the first day of a new moon as well as all the fifteenth day following the moon’s waxing, throughout the day from sunrise till the sixth hour. But during [the moon] waning phase, the stone pours forth its virtue before the day dawns. The other two species are called cephalic and hepatic, and their virtues are not small. The cephalic is so called for having been found inside the head of the tortoise. It is therefore useful for headaches and resists lightning. The hepatic, too, takes its name from the place where it is found, the liver. This stone, if ground and drunk with water, prevents quartan fevers, if taken just before its onset. These stones are also called drome: if carried with peony roots, they render the wearer highly powerful.

Swallow; if this image is carved on a stone called celonites, it has the virtue of making and preserving peace and concord among men.
