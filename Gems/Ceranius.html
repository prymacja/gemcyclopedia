Ceranius, also called Cerraolus or Ombria, is a pyramidal stone that falls from clouds near lightning. ¹ Germany has the finest kind; Spain’s is next and shines like fire. It has crystalline saffron-colored and pyrite-colored kinds, while its black form is disputed. ¹ Worn, it prevents drowning, lightning, and tornadoes, grants victory in legal cases and battles, and makes dreams sweet and pleasant. ¹

Generic

Description

Ceranius is pyramidal and falls from clouds near places where lightning has struck. Germany yields the finest kind, while Spain’s is next in rank and shines like flames. One account describes a crystalline kind tinged with saffron and another colored like pyrite. ¹ A proposed black kind is disputed: some describe it, while another tradition says the stone has various colors.

Properties

Worn, Ceranius saves its bearer from drowning and protects against lightning and tornadoes. ¹ It grants victory in legal cases and battles and makes dreams sweet and pleasant. ¹

Ceranius or Cerraolus is a pyramidal-shaped stone. This stone is of two types: one is crystalline tinged with a saffron color, the other is pyrite colored. It said that it falls from the clouds, close to where lightning has fallen. The type found in Germany is the best. The Spanish comes second, and it is resplendent like the flames of a fire. Socatus proposes the existence of another type that is black. Evax contradicts this, saying that there are various colors. It is very hard and has great virtue. It indeed saves the wearer from drowning, nor can he be hit by lightning or tornadoes. It renders man victorious in legal cases and in battles. It also renders dreams sweet and pleasant.

Ombria is the same as Ceranius, of which we spoke above.
