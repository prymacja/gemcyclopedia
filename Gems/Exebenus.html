Exebenus

NamesExebenus; ¹ ² Exebonos. ²
AppearanceBright, shining, fair white. ¹ ²

Exebenus, also called Exebonos, is a bright white stone used by goldsmiths in polishing gold. ¹ Ground and drunk, it heals madness and stomach pains; ² tied to the thigh, it eases bladder pain and hastens childbirth.

Generic

Description

Exebenus is brilliant white. ¹ Goldsmiths use it to polish gold; ¹ in another account it is used to burnish gold when smelted with it. ²

Properties

Ground and drunk, Exebenus heals madness and stomach pains. ² It is useful to a fetus still in the womb. When tied to the thigh, it dissolves bladder pain, hastens birth, and halts venereal pleasures. ²

Exebonos or Exebenus is a white and fair stone, used by goldsmiths to burnish gold, if smelted with it. Ground and drunk, it heals madness, and similarly those who suffer from stomach pains. It is useful to the fetus still in utero.

Tied to the femur, it dissolves bladder pain, hastens birth, and halts venereal pleasures.
