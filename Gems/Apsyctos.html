NamesApsyctos. ¹ ²

Apsyctos is a heavy black stone marked with red veins. ¹ After being heated by fire, it retains warmth for almost seven days; ¹ ² one account gives the full span as seven days. ²

Generic

Description

Apsyctos is heavy and black, crossed by red or blood-red veins. ¹ ²

Properties

When heated by fire, Apsyctos retains the warmth for nearly seven days; ¹ ² another account says it keeps the heat for seven full days. ²

Sources

1. St. Isidore of Seville — Etymologies XVI.xi.2

Open cited source

Apsyctos is black and heavy, and marked with red veins. When heated by fire, this stone retains the warmth for seven days. — Etymologies XVI.xi.2

2. Marbodus of Rennes — De lapidibus

Open cited source

Mid gems Apsyctos is not last in place,
And sanguine veins its ebon surface grace:
A pond'rous stone, once heated at the flame.
The fire conceiv'd scarce seven full days can tame.
