Classification notes

  • Ordinary appearance, scent, or routine material uses are omitted when they do not describe a distinctive power.
  • The Healing article begins with an index of named conditions; a gem can appear under each condition its entry addresses.
  • Human fertility and childbirth are grouped separately from powers affecting animals, crops, and livestock.
  • Source citations remain in each linked gem article so that the Power pages can serve as concise, navigable subject indexes.
