Exorcism

This page groups gem powers used to expel or repel spirits and demons and to resist enchantments, phantasms, and related afflictions.

Driving away spirits and demons

Amethyst

A bear drives demons away and protects the wearer from drunkenness.

See Amethyst for the full entry and source citations.

Pontica

Pontica also drives demons away.

See Pontica for the full entry and source citations.

Chrysolite

Set in gold and worn on the left arm, Chrysolite drives away fiends and night terrors; pierced and threaded with donkey hairs, it drives away demons.

See Chrysolite for the full entry and source citations.

Chrysoprase

Airy spirits cannot mock the sufferer, who expels foam from the mouth.

For possession, pour water over Chrysoprase while saying: “O water, I pour you over this stone in that power by which God made the sun as well as the hastening moon.” Give the water to the possessed person, even if he is unwilling to drink it. It tortures and weakens the demon for a day. If the demon is gentle, it will depart. A bitter and fierce demon is recognized when the sufferer is unwilling to speak, remains willfully mute and does not laugh readily, arches the hands, or spits foam. This stone has little power to expel such a demon, but it torments and weakens it, and it may be driven out when God wills.

See Chrysoprase for the full entry and source citations.

Coral

It drives away ghosts, demons, illusions, and nightmares, restrains lightning, winds, tempests, and whirlwinds, and protects against wild beasts.

See Coral for the full entry and source citations.

Kinocetus

Kinocetus casts out demons.

See Kinocetus for the full entry and source citations.

Ruby

Airy spirits cannot complete their phantasms where the stone is present.

See Ruby for the full entry and source citations.

Sapphire

To torment and expel an evil spirit, enclose the stone with earth in a leather bag, suspend it from the neck, and say the prescribed prayer.

See Sapphire for the full entry and source citations.

Topaz

Set in gold, it dispels phantasms, expels stupidity, and gives wisdom.

Airy spirits recoil from it, and it holds the powers of the day’s seven hours.

Strung on hairs from an ass’s tail and worn as a bracelet on the left arm, it makes even the mightiest demons quail.

Set in gold and carried in the left hand, Crisoletus drives away demons, nightmares, and melancholy visions. It weakens the enchantments of evil women and ends their charms. Bore a hole through the stone and draw a hair from an ass through it to strengthen its power against demons.

See Topaz for the full entry and source citations.

Sardachat

The passage links Sardachat with Agate, which myth-makers call a driver-away of evil things.

See Sardachat for the full entry and source citations.

Sardonyx

This also removes great wrath, stupidity, and illiteracy, and the devil flees its purity.

See Sardonyx for the full entry and source citations.

Resisting enchantments and dispelling phantasms

Antiphates

Antiphates is useful against enchantments.

See Antiphates for the full entry and source citations.

Catochites

Taken in the amount of a scruple, it resists the magic arts.

See Catochites for the full entry and source citations.

Chalcedony

Pierced with Emery (smyris) and hung around the neck, Chalcedony is said to dispel fanciful illusions arising from melancholy.

See Chalcedony for the full entry and source citations.

Crystal

Hung over a sleeper, it dispels bad dreams and frees those who have been charmed.

See Crystal for the full entry and source citations.

Diamond

The diamond repels poison, sorcery, enchantment, and evil arts. It reveals poisons and dispels vain fears, madness, frenzy, and demonic possession. The devil also disdains it.

See Diamond for the full entry and source citations.

Emerald

It halts demonic illusions and tempests, and relieves tertian fever.

See Emerald for the full entry and source citations.

Emetrem

Emetrem is used to make charms that ward off the evil eye.

See Emetrem for the full entry and source citations.

Epistrites

Worn over the heart with the proper ligatures and spells, Epistrites keeps its bearer safe, restrains sedition, and wards off misfortune.

See Epistrites for the full entry and source citations.

Hyacinth

For madness from delusions or magic words, warm rye bread and cut a cross into its upper crust without cutting all the way through. Draw the stone down through the cut while saying, “May God, who cast every precious stone from the devil when he transgressed his command, now cast from you, N., every delusion and magic word, and free you from this madness.” Draw the stone across the warm bread while saying, “As the splendor the devil possessed was taken from him for his transgression, so may the madness tormenting you, N., through delusions and magic words be taken from you and disappear.” Give the patient the bread around the traced area to eat.

See Hyacinth for the full entry and source citations.

Isciscos

Isciscos withstands poisoners, especially magicians.

See Isciscos for the full entry and source citations.

Jet

Its smoke is said to drive away serpents, demons, and spells.

Jet is used against dropsy, stomach and belly disorders, and melancholy’s phantasms.

See Jet for the full entry and source citations.

Lignites

Hung around a child’s neck, Lignites protects the child from charms and spells of witches.

See Lignites for the full entry and source citations.

Malachite

Malachite protects its bearer from harm and guards infants’ cradles against mischance and witchcraft. It protects children from accidents and hurtful phantasms so that they may grow up in prosperity.

See Malachite for the full entry and source citations.

Jasper

Carrying Jasper disperses phantasms and threats that appear in dreams, especially thunder and lightning. A mother should keep it on her hand from childbirth through the child’s infancy to guard both from malign spirits.

Remedies fever phantasms.

See Jasper for the full entry and source citations.

Magnet

Moistened with the saliva of one troubled by madness or phantasms, then rubbed over the forehead and crown while the prescribed prayer is spoken, the stone restores the sufferer’s senses.

See Magnet for the full entry and source citations.
