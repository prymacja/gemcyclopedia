Protecting animals from predators, pests, and poison

Aetites

The stone repels poisonous animals and protects eagles’ eggs and eaglets.

Standing eagle — Carved on Ethices stone and set in a lead ring. — Lets the wearer catch many fish; no wild beast harms him, and he is loved by all.

Standing eagle — Engraved on Ethices and set in a lead ring. — Makes the wearer loved by all and causes animals to obey him.

See Aetites for the full entry and source citations.

Amber

When burnt, it drives away serpents.

See Amber for the full entry and source citations.

Amethyst

Passing amethyst over the flesh heals a spider bite, and the stone repels serpents and adders.

Water in which the stone has been washed makes barren women fruitful when drunk.

See Amethyst for the full entry and source citations.

Chalcantum

Chalcantum’s astringent force is so concentrated that, when sprinkled into the mouths of lions or bears, it prevents them from biting.

See Chalcantum for the full entry and source citations.

Diamond

Worn on the left arm, it gives victory and courage and guards against enemies, wild beasts, savage men, weapons, quarrels, phantoms, and night terrors.

See Diamond for the full entry and source citations.

Falcones

Swallowed, it is poisonous to all animals.

See Falcones for the full entry and source citations.

Garatides

The stone is recognized by a trial with honey and insects. Anoint a person’s body with honey and place him where flies and bees gather while holding Garatides; they leave him untouched. When the stone is laid aside, the insects molest him. Another account specifies flies and wasps, and says that they fall upon the honey as soon as the wearer removes the stone.

See Garatides for the full entry and source citations.

Gerades

Gerades protects men from birds of prey.

See Gerades for the full entry and source citations.

Jasper

Where a serpent breathes, place Jasper: it weakens the breath and causes the serpent to stop breathing there.

Pallios: Wild animals and monsters fear it.

See Jasper for the full entry and source citations.

Jet

Its smoke is said to drive away serpents, demons, and spells.

See Jet for the full entry and source citations.

Malachite

Malachite protects its bearer from harm and guards the cradles of infants against mischance and witchcraft.

See Malachite for the full entry and source citations.

Tephrias

When worn bound on the body, Tephrias protects against serpents.

See Tephrias for the full entry and source citations.

Turquoise

Its appearance strengthens sight, and its bearer is protected from outward and evil accidents.

See Turquoise for the full entry and source citations.

Protecting fields and crops

Balagius

Touching the four corners of a house, garden, or vineyard with it is said to protect them from lightning, storms, and worms.

See Balagius for the full entry and source citations.

Coral

It drives away ghosts, demons, illusions, and nightmares, restrains lightning, winds, tempests, and whirlwinds, and protects against wild beasts.

Coral brings abundance to cultivated land. Powdered and sprinkled with water upon herbs and trees, it multiplies their fruit. Hung among fruit trees or mixed with seed in the field, it increases fertility and keeps away hail and rain.

See Coral for the full entry and source citations.

Epistrites

It drives locusts, harmful birds, clouds, hailstorms, and tornadoes away from fertile land and crops.

See Epistrites for the full entry and source citations.

Hephaestite

It shields fields from locusts, hail, whirlwinds, and rust.

See Hephaestite for the full entry and source citations.

Hunting, fishing, and attracting animals

Crystal

Woman holding a bird in one hand and a fish in the other — Engraved on crystal. — Helps the bearer catch birds.

See Crystal for the full entry and source citations.

Liparea

When burned, Liparea calls all animals. Worn by a hunter in the woods, it draws the pursued game to his spear, so that he needs neither hounds nor snares.

So long as they remain in its presence, dogs and hunters cannot harm them. It is also said that while an animal gazes upon Liparea, neither dog nor hunter can see it.

See Liparea for the full entry and source citations.

Livestock health, milk, and birth

Galactite

To increase milk in ewes and protect them from disease, crush Galactite with salt and mix it with lustral water. Another account directs that the mixture be sprinkled at night around the sheepfold. It makes the ewes’ udders abound with milk and drives away murrain or scab. One account adds that the sheep become fertile and free of mange, and says the stone also cures mange in people.

See Galactite for the full entry and source citations.

Onyx

For stomach pain, prepare wine with Onyx as above, make a broth from that wine, hens’ eggs, and flour, and eat it often.

To aid cattle during pestilence, hold Onyx over steaming water so its sweat enters the water, then leave the stone in the water for three days.

See Onyx for the full entry and source citations.

Orites

Subtype I: Black and Round: Ground and mixed with rose oil, heals animal wounds and noxious bites.

See Orites for the full entry and source citations.

Topaz

Chrysolite strengthens newborn sheep and other animals when present at birth, helping them move sooner.

See Topaz for the full entry and source citations.
