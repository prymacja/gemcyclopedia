Fever and ague

Aquilinus Limphaticus

Hung around the neck or carried on the person, Aquilinus Limphaticus wards off the distress of quartan fever.

Asinus

Taken in wine, it keeps away quartan fever.

Bolus Armenus

It is also used against fever and fluxes of the belly, and helps bloody expectoration, splenic ailments, and other disorders of the stomach.

Borax

For snakebites, fever, and ailments of the stomach and kidneys, drink water in which Borax has been placed.

Celidonius

Wrapped in yellow linen and worn around the neck, the stone banishes fevers and obstructive watery humors.

Chryselectrum

Held in the hand, the stone reduces the heat of fever.

Daemonius

Daemonius is prescribed against fever and expels poison.

Emerald

Worn about the neck, it cures hemitertian fever and epilepsy, strengthens weak sight, preserves the eyes, and improves memory. When sudden pestilential attacks come, moisten it in the mouth and repeatedly touch it to the body. It halts demonic illusions and tempests, and relieves tertian fever.

Enhydros

Enhydros heals burning fevers and is beneficial to those suffering from fever.

Garnet

Some say it protects its wearer from plague.

Hiena

Carried on the person, it guards against quartan ague and gout.

Jasper

Heals fevers and dropsy. Remedies fever phantasms.

Lapis lazuli

It is also a sure remedy for black bile, quartan fever, and fainting caused by black bile vapors.

Limacie

Hung around the neck, Limacie frees its bearer from fever.

Onyx

For a strong fever, soak Onyx in vinegar for five days. Remove the stone and season all food with the vinegar. The fever will cease. The stone’s good heat mixed with the vinegar’s heat drives away the noxious humors that cause fever. To aid cattle during pestilence, hold Onyx over steaming water so its sweat enters the water, then leave the stone in the water for three days. Give the water to the cows to drink, sprinkle their fodder with it, and mix bran with it for them to eat.

Pearl

Cooked in food, they heal quartan fevers. They comfort the heart, aid fainting and epilepsy, stop the flux of the belly, and when taken with sugar help against pestilential fevers.

Prasius

For a burning fever, roll Prasius in a little rye-bread dough, tie it in cloth, and keep it over the navel for three days and nights. The fever departs.

Ruby

It also drives away pestilential and poisonous air, quiets lust, preserves bodily health, banishes evil thoughts, reconciles friends who quarrel, and increases prosperity.

Sard

To avert pestilence, carry the stone.

Topaz

For fever, hold the stone over steaming wine, mix its sweat into the wine, drink it warm, and briefly place the stone in the mouth. Repeat often. Held in the hand, it dispels feverish heat. To cure fever, make three moderately sized trenches in soft bread with Topaz and fill them with pure wine, adding more as it disappears. Look at the face reflected in the wine and say: “I look at myself, just as the cherubim and seraphim look at God in a mirror, so that these fevers might be cast from me.” Repeat often.

Aetites

It also prevents frequent epileptic attacks and is said to heal epilepsy.

Agate

A person afflicted with epilepsy or lunacy may wear it against the skin and use water in which the stone has rested to prepare food and drink. For epilepsy, place the stone in water at the full moon for three days.

Amber

Its smoke also drives away poisonous creatures and its vapors repel epilepsy.

Celidonius

Worn around the neck, it heals epilepsy and jaundice.

Chrysoprase

Carried by one who has epilepsy, it lessens the disease.

Emerald

Worn about the neck, it cures hemitertian fever and epilepsy, strengthens weak sight, preserves the eyes, and improves memory. For an epileptic attack, hold it to the sufferer. To cure epilepsy, look upon it on nine successive mornings and recite the prescribed prayer.

Jet

One account adds that it cures epilepsy and that jet mixed with a stag’s marrow heals a snakebite.

Limphicus

Displayed, Limphicus frees a person from seizures.

Onyx

Onyx worn around the neck also prevents falls in those who suffer from epilepsy.

Pearl

They comfort the heart, aid fainting and epilepsy, stop the flux of the belly, and when taken with sugar help against pestilential fevers.

Sulfur

When sulfur is set on fire, its vapor is said to cure the comitial sickness. If sulfur is placed in a goblet of wine and carried about with hot coals beneath it, the reflection of the blaze makes a person glow with the eerie pallor of a corpse.

Poisoning, venom, and bites

Aetites

The stone repels poisonous animals and protects eagles’ eggs and eaglets. It tests suspected poison: when given to one about to eat poisoned food, it prevents him from swallowing. Once the stone is removed, he can swallow. Another test says that a guilty poisoner chokes while the stone lies in the dish, but an innocent person eats freely.

Agapis

It has marvelous properties against scorpions stings and vipers bites.

Agate

For venom from a spider or other vermin, heat the stone in the sun or on a hot tile and lay it upon the painful place. A linen cloth dipped in the water and laid over the bite draws out the poison and heals the wound. A man holding a viper repels its venom. The constellation Serpentarium engraved on Sicilian agate doubles its power against poison. The Sicilian kind also counters viper and scorpion venom. Indian blood-spotted: Preserves sight and counters thirst and poison. Lion-coloured: Ground with water and applied to the place, wards off venom from viper and scorpion bites and like poisons. Cretan red-and-gold: Counters swallowed poison and venomous snakebites. It also repels venomous creatures, strengthens sight, grants victory and strength, and repels tempests and lightning. Cretan coral-like: Sharpens sight, quenches thirst, counters poison, and gives grace, eloquence, preservation, and strength.

Amandinus

Amandinus counteracts or mitigates all poison and brings victory over adversaries. It also grants understanding of prophecy and the interpretation of dreams and riddles, enabling the interpreter to solve the questions set before him.

Amber

Its smoke also drives away poisonous creatures and its vapors repel epilepsy. Amber halts flux of the belly, remedies infirmities of the throat, repels poison, and holds loose teeth fast.

Amethyst

Passing amethyst over the flesh heals a spider bite, and the stone repels serpents and adders. Amethyst extinguishes poison, protects soldiers, and brings victory over the enemy.

Asinus

Asinus resists all poisonous animals and heals snakebites. It also makes harmless poisoned water that has already been drunk, helps women in childbirth, and aids the expulsion of a fetus that died in the womb.

Beryl

When a person has eaten or drunk poison, grate a moderate amount of beryl into spring water or other water. Drink it immediately and continue once daily on an empty stomach for five days. The poison will pass through nausea or the bowels.

Bezoar

Taken inward in a dram, bezoar counteracts and eliminates any poison. It is esteemed as the first remedy against poison.

Borax

Swallowed after poison has been drunk, Borax braces the intestines, extinguishes the poison’s force, and carries it out through the lower parts. It is also said to cleanse the bowels of filth and excrement. For snakebites, fever, and ailments of the stomach and kidneys, drink water in which Borax has been placed.

Chrysoprase

Wherever the stone is, death-bearing poison loses its strength.

Daemonius

Daemonius is prescribed against fever and expels poison.

Diamond

The diamond repels poison, sorcery, enchantment, and evil arts. It reveals poisons and dispels vain fears, madness, frenzy, and demonic possession.

Draconites

The stone is said to dispel poison, especially venom from snakes and other poisonous creatures. It also grants victory, courage, and invincibility.

Emerald

The emerald counters poisonous bites, especially those of a rabid dog. Taken in powdered form, it serves against leprosy, kidney stones, and poison.

Falcones

Swallowed, it is poisonous to all animals.

Haematite

Dissolved in water, Haematite allays the pain of a poisonous serpent’s bite or an asp’s dart.

Heliotrope

It checks the flow of blood, expels poisons, and protects against treachery.

Hyacinth

Saphirinus has a special power against poison.

Jet

One account adds that it cures epilepsy and that jet mixed with a stag’s marrow heals a snakebite.

Kenne

Kenne is good against poisons.

Magnet

Placed over a wound made by poisoned iron, it heals it.

Nusae

When both kinds are shut together in the presence of poison, they burn the hand of anyone who touches them. In the presence of poison, the whitish kind takes on varied colors.

Ophites

When Ophites is hung by the neck, it heals headaches and serpent bites.

Oristes

When Oristes is rubbed with rose oil, it preserves its bearer from misfortune and the harmful bites of reptiles. When worn by a woman, it prevents conception. If she is already pregnant, it causes miscarriage.

Orites

Subtype I: Black and Round: Ground and mixed with rose oil, heals animal wounds and noxious bites.

Pyrophilus

Pyrophilus brings victory and protects its bearer from poison.

Ranius

Ranius resists poison, as does Bolus Armenus.

Reyben

A plaster of Reyben treats scorpion and weasel bites; its powder taken in drink is used for the bite of a rabid dog.

See Reyben for the full entry and source citations.

Ruby

The carbuncle’s chief virtue is to disperse poison in the air or vapor. It also drives away pestilential and poisonous air, quiets lust, preserves bodily health, banishes evil thoughts, reconciles friends who quarrel, and increases prosperity.

Solis Gemma

Solis Gemma is held to counter deadly poisons that have been drunk.

Talc Alchimicus

Talc Alchimicus is used through sublimation to make the worst poisons.

Topaz

Topaz resists heat and poison. When poison is in food or drink, the stone sweats if brought near it.

Ydrinus

It drives away poisonous worms and heals their bites. If ingested, it breaks bladder stones.

Dropsy and excess fluid

Cambnites

When bound to the left arm, it heals dropsy.

Celidonius

Wrapped in yellow linen and worn around the neck, the stone banishes fevers and obstructive watery humors.

Jasper

Heals fevers and dropsy.

Jet

Jet is used against dropsy, stomach and belly disorders, and melancholy’s phantasms.

Kabrates

It protects against evil accidents and heals dropsy. When tied to the left arm, the possibly identical Cambnites also heals dropsy.

Magnet

Ground and taken with honey or mead, it cures dropsy.

Sindicus

To treat dropsy, bind the stone to the sick person for three hours. Then expose it to the sun for three hours: the stone pours forth the foul water it has received and becomes clean again.

Ydrinus

Ydrinus heals rheum and excessive humidity in the human body. For dropsy, the sufferer stands in the sun with the stone for three hours. Sweating then expels foul water and restores the body to its former health.

Bleeding and disorders of the blood

Alabandina

Its virtue is that of bringing on the flux of blood.

Alabandine

Alabandine is said to excite and increase the blood.

See Alabandine for the full entry and source citations.

Amber

Taken inwardly, it increases the flow of urine and quickens menstruation as well as easing childbirth.

Bolus Armenus

Bolus Armenus overcomes looseness of the bowels, especially bleeding associated with dysentery and menstruation. It is also used against fever and fluxes of the belly, and helps bloody expectoration, splenic ailments, and other disorders of the stomach.

Bolus Armenus constrains and hardens the womb and staunches blood from any part of the body.

See Bolus Armenus for the full entry and source citations.

Carnelian

Carnelian restrains bleeding, especially menstrual and hemorrhoidal bleeding. Another account also assigns it a power against dysentery. One account says the raw-flesh-colored variety stops bleeding both externally and internally. For a nosebleed, heat wine, place Carnelian in it, and give the wine to the sufferer to drink. The bleeding will cease.

Haematite

Grind it in water until the liquid turns blood-red, then use it to treat bloody sputum, dysentery, menses, and other fluxes of blood. Crushed and drunk with water, it heals fluxes of the bladder and bowels, menstruation, and bloody saliva. Another account also gives a repeated dose dissolved in wine to stop looseness.

Heliotrope

The stone appears red and the sun turns blood-red, as in an eclipse.

Jasper

Jasper reduces or stops bleeding, including menstrual flow.

Jet

Its odor or smoke provokes menstruation. When washed in water, or when its fumes are applied from below, it brings on menstruation. Another account ascribes this effect to its smell.

Ostracites

Taken in a drink, it halts bleeding.

Pearl

Pearls are said to relieve difficulty in breathing, heart attacks, fainting, bleeding, jaundice, and diarrhea.

Sapphire

It stops bleeding.

Sard

It prevents terrible dreams, increases wealth and cheerfulness, restrains bleeding, and grants victory over enemies. One account gives it no virtue beyond beauty and the power to break Onyx’s malign influence.

Topaz

It is a remedy for hemorrhoids and attacks of lunacy. When thrown into a boiling cauldron, it stills the bubbling, and it relieves hemorrhoids. Topaz heals delirium and injured minds, hemorrhoids, and lunatic suffering. Bound above a wound, it stops bleeding. It also halts sudden death.

Zigrites

Worn around the neck, Zigrites reduces bleeding and dispels delirium.

Zingutte

Zingutte is said to staunch blood.

See Zingutte for the full entry and source citations.

Zirites

When hung around the neck, Zirites halts the flux of blood and insanity of mind.

Chemites

Coral

It helps abscesses of the stomach, stops corrosion of the gums, heals putrid ulcers, counters harmful medicines, and, when its shavings or scrapings are drunk with wine, helps those who pass small gravel in their urine.

Crystal

Emerald

Carried on the person, it relieves weakness and illness of the heart, stomach, and side. When sudden pestilential attacks come, moisten it in the mouth and repeatedly touch it to the body. Bound over a wound for three days, it kills worms.

Exacolitus

Mixed with wine and drunk, it is said to help colic and internal pains. In another preparation, grind the stone, steep it in wine, and drink it to heal colics and iliac passions.

Exebenus

Ground and drunk, Exebenus heals madness and stomach pains. It is useful to a fetus still in the womb. When tied to the thigh, it dissolves bladder pain, hastens birth, and halts venereal pleasures.

Haematite

Jet

Jet is used against dropsy, stomach and belly disorders, and melancholy’s phantasms.

Ligurius

The stone relieves pain in the stomach, chest, and belly, and is said to cure jaundice and diarrhea. For severe stomach pain, place it briefly in wine, beer, or water, then remove it. Let the patient drink this liquid in moderation after eating a little, never while fasting, and repeat this treatment for fifteen days. It is for stomach pain alone: another person who drinks it may suffer injury to the heart and a splitting headache, and may not survive. One account says that the stone should be worn to arrest diarrhea.

Lime

For worms in a wound, mix lime with twice as much chalk and enough wine or water to make a thin cement. Apply it to the wound with a feather each day through the fifth day. Then crush aloe with one-third as much myrrh, mix them with fresh wax to make a plaster, place it on hempen cloth, and bind it over the wound for twelve days. The lime’s heat, tempered by the chalk’s cold and by the wine’s heat and sharpness, kills the worms.

Lyncis

The stone heals those troubled by kidney stones, stomach pain, flux of the belly, and jaundice.

Onyx

For stomach pain, prepare wine with Onyx as above, make a broth from that wine, hens’ eggs, and flour, and eat it often. It purges and heals the stomach.

Pearl

Pearls are said to relieve difficulty in breathing, heart attacks, fainting, bleeding, jaundice, and diarrhea. They comfort the heart, aid fainting and epilepsy, stop the flux of the belly, and when taken with sugar help against pestilential fevers.

Sapphire

Ydrinus

It drives away poisonous worms and heals their bites. If ingested, it breaks bladder stones.

Eyes and sight

Agate

It also repels venomous creatures, strengthens sight, grants victory and strength, and repels tempests and lightning. Cretan coral-like: Sharpens sight, quenches thirst, counters poison, and gives grace, eloquence, preservation, and strength.

Balagius

Ground and drunk with water, it heals illnesses of the eyes and aids disorders of the liver.

Beryl

Beryl also counters sloth, pain in the liver and side, shortness of breath, belching, and watery eyes. Water in which beryl has rested for a time is good for weak sight. When drunk, it dispels sighs and heals liver disorders.

Calaminaris

When ground nine times in vinegar and finely pulverized with bird’s blood, Calaminaris makes a salve for inverted eyelids.

Carnelian

A seated man before a woman whose hair reaches her thighs and whose eyes are raised.

Celidonius

They grant eloquence and persuasive power, soften the anger of rulers, and refresh weary sight.

Worn about the neck, it cures hemitertian fever and epilepsy, strengthens weak sight, preserves the eyes, and improves memory.

Emetrem

Mixed with honey, it makes an ointment for eye maladies. A well-beaten egg white, or honey and pomegranate juice, added to the water mixture, heals styes on the eyelids and hazy sight. It also treats dimness of sight caused by moisture and roughness of the eyelids.

Heliotrope

Hyacinth

Jasper

When bound to the left arm, it heals dropsy.

Celidonius

The stones, borne with due reverence, aid wasting illness and disorders described as lunacy or mania. Wrapped in yellow linen and worn around the neck, the stone banishes fevers and obstructive watery humors.

Chalcedony

Worn against bare skin over a vein, Chalcedony turns away infirmities, strengthens the blood, and gives a mind strong against wrath.

Chrysoprase

Carried by one who has epilepsy, it lessens the disease.

Crystal

Its watery nature draws away harmful humors and improves sight. For pain or ailment of the heart, stomach, or belly, warm crystal in the sun and pour water over it.

Diamond

The diamond withstands fire and iron, scratches iron and other gems, and its splinters can cut gemstones, iron, and steel. One kind pierces iron but sticks fast in steel. Lead also destroys and softens it by virtue of its quicksilver content. It is softened or shattered by fresh goat's blood; one preparation requires a goat that has long drunk wine with wild parsley or eaten mountain fenugreek. Another tradition names fresh, warm stag's blood. Crystal and gold-veined kinds split after long immersion in goat's blood and repeated hammer blows; elsewhere the stone yields to an iron hammer after soaking in fresh, warm he-goat's blood. These methods belong to different kinds and accounts. Engraved on a diamond, the head of a long-bearded man with blood about his neck gives victory and courage and protects the body from bodily harm.

Drosolithus

When placed close to fire, Drosolithus emits something like sweat.

Emerald

Carried on the person, it relieves weakness and illness of the heart, stomach, and side. When sudden pestilential attacks come, moisten it in the mouth and repeatedly touch it to the body.

Galactite

To increase milk in ewes and protect them from disease, crush Galactite with salt and mix it with lustral water. Before sunrise, carry it around the fold. Another account directs that the mixture be sprinkled at night around the sheepfold.

Hyacinth

They guard travelers from noxious air and contagious disease, defend against lightning and enemies, bring honor and wealth, and help secure a favorable hearing from princes. If bodily weakness prevents eating rye bread, bless warm unleavened bread with the stone and the same words.

Jasper

Heals fevers and dropsy. It calms disorders of the humors in the heart, kidneys, or elsewhere. Green healer with herbs

Jet

Jet is used against dropsy, stomach and belly disorders, and melancholy’s phantasms.

Kabrates

It protects against evil accidents and heals dropsy. When tied to the left arm, the possibly identical Cambnites also heals dropsy.

Lacteus

When placed upon rheumatic eyes, it halts the flow of humor. It also helps cure the beginning of abscesses in inflamed eyes.

Lapis lazuli

Prepared as physicians direct, lapis lazuli cures melancholic illnesses. It is also a sure remedy for black bile, quartan fever, and fainting caused by black bile vapors.

Magnet

Ground and taken with honey or mead, it cures dropsy.

Melites

Melites is useful for preparing various medicines, though no particular remedy is specified.

Obtalius

Obtalius preserves the eyes from various illnesses. When carried with a laurel leaf and the proper charms, it sharpens the wearer’s sight and darkens the sight of those around, until they are unable to see.

Okitokius

Liquefy Okitokius with echium’s juice. Mix it with the blood of a swift, the head of an omide, and water, then place the mixture in a glass vessel. When a person dips a finger into the unguent and touches any hard wood, metal, or stone, that object immediately breaks.

Onyx

Placed beneath a sick eye, it enters of its own accord, circles the eye, and extinguishes any illness it finds there. It also tempers noxious and contrary humors. The stone’s good heat mixed with the vinegar’s heat drives away the noxious humors that cause fever.

Ophthalmius

Ophthalmius chases ailments from the eyes and gives its bearer the sharpest sight.

Ophthalmus

Ophthalmus protects the wearer against all bad eye diseases.

Pavonius

Mix Pavonius into a drink with a bit of sweat. The person whose sweat it is and who drinks the mixture is made to burn with love.

Pyrites

The yellow kind benefits the eyes and is useful in medicines for sore eyes and many other illnesses.

Radaim

Radaim enables its possessor to obtain whatever he wishes. It heals its wearer, brings him honor, and aids him in governing.

Ruby

For any illness that has changed a person’s humors, place the carbuncle on the navel at midnight. Remove it as soon as the person feels warmth from the stone or the slightest motion in the body. If left longer, its power penetrates the whole body and dries it, including the internal organs.

Samius

For other eye ailments, grind the stone with milk and lay it over the eyes.

Sapphire

Ground and mixed with milk, sapphire heals itching and fungal disease when applied to the affected places. It relieves illness, internal heat, sweating, ulcers, eye problems, headaches, and speech impairment. A ram, or a bearded man's head, heals and frees the bearer from many infirmities, prison, and burdens.

Sard

One account prescribes Sard against illness and gives specific methods. For severe head illness, tie it atop the head in a hat, cloth, or leather pouch and recite the accompanying prayer asking God to cut away the illness and restore sound understanding.

Sardonyx

After sweating from an acute illness and beginning to recover, place it on a finger in a ring to prevent the illness from returning.

Silenite

Silonites is said to cure consumption and long illness.

See Silenite for the full entry and source citations.

Sindicus

To treat dropsy, bind the stone to the sick person for three hours.

Topaz

For ailments of the spleen or internal corruption, place Topaz in straight wine for five days. Remove the stone and boil the wine. Hold the stone over its steam until it sweats into the wine, then place it briefly in the warm wine.

Varach

Varach remedies every kind of flux, especially the flow of blood. Mixed with quicksilver, it makes algala. Another account likewise values it for stopping every sort of flux.

Vernix

Another account also commends it to those suffering melancholia and ailments of these organs.

Ydrinus

Ydrinus heals rheum and excessive humidity in the human body. For dropsy, the sufferer stands in the sun with the stone for three hours.
