Pregnancy and miscarriage

Aetites

Worn on the left arm, Aetites strengthens pregnant women, prevents miscarriage, and lessens the dangers of childbirth. Placed on the thigh during labor, it removes danger and speeds the birth. When the hard male and soft female stones are joined, they hasten delivery, though they may force out part of the womb if they are not promptly removed.

See Aetites for the full entry and source citations.

Asinus

It also makes harmless poisoned water that has already been drunk, helps women in childbirth, and aids the expulsion of a fetus that died in the womb.

See Asinus for the full entry and source citations.

Beryl

It also aids pregnant women, preventing miscarriage and birth pains.

See Beryl for the full entry and source citations.

Jasper

Jasper reduces or stops bleeding, including menstrual flow.

A mother should keep it on her hand from childbirth through the child’s infancy to guard both from malign spirits.

Traditions differ on conception and pregnancy. Some say Jasper prevents conception yet assists childbirth. The green kind with saffron veins is especially helpful to a pregnant woman. Another account calls belief in Jasper’s protection of pregnant women superstition.

Worn at the neck, Jasper eases labor, guards against misfortune and night phantoms, and gains strength from blessing and silver.

Green with saffron veins: Especially helps pregnant women and aids childbirth.

See Jasper for the full entry and source citations.

Oristes

When worn by a woman, it prevents conception. If she is already pregnant, it causes miscarriage.

See Oristes for the full entry and source citations.

Orites

Subtype III: Studded Iron-Like Kind: Worn, it prevents conception. Hung around the neck, it causes a pregnant woman to miscarry.

See Orites for the full entry and source citations.

Peranites

Peranites is said to be good for pregnant women.

See Peranites for the full entry and source citations.

Samius

Taken in drink, Samius cures dizziness, calms the mind, and prevents miscarriage.

If Samius is bound to the hand of a woman in childbirth, it hinders the birth and keeps the child in the womb.

See Samius for the full entry and source citations.

Labor and childbirth

Amber

It helps pregnant women to an easy birth. Taken inwardly, it increases the flow of urine and quickens menstruation as well as easing childbirth.

See Amber for the full entry and source citations.

Exebenus

It is useful to a fetus still in the womb. When tied to the thigh, it dissolves bladder pain, hastens birth, and halts venereal pleasures.

See Exebenus for the full entry and source citations.

Hyacinth

The stone also eases childbirth and drives away nightmares.

One warning stands apart: a contrary tradition holds that Hyacinth can cause miscarriage in pregnant women.

See Hyacinth for the full entry and source citations.

Magnet

Held in the hand during labor, it eases childbirth.

See Magnet for the full entry and source citations.

Poeanites

Pregnant women trust Poeanites for protection during childbirth, especially at the dangerous moment of delivery.

See Poeanites for the full entry and source citations.

Samothracia

Its smoke is useful against a mother’s labor pains.

See Samothracia for the full entry and source citations.

Sard

To aid a woman unable to give birth, rub the stone over both thighs while saying the prescribed prayer, hold it at the birth opening and say the second prayer, then tie it in a belt and cinch the belt around her.

See Sard for the full entry and source citations.

Conception and fertility

Amethyst

Water in which the stone has been washed makes barren women fruitful when drunk.

See Amethyst for the full entry and source citations.

Gasidana

Gasidana conceives. When the stone is shaken, the fetus appears within it.

See Gasidana for the full entry and source citations.

Peantes

An older account says that the female stone can conceive at certain times and bear a stone like itself. Another interpretation says that “female” refers not to the stone’s own conception, but to its virtues helping women to conceive and give birth.

See Peantes for the full entry and source citations.

Menstruation and female cycles

Bolus Armenus

Bolus Armenus overcomes looseness of the bowels, especially bleeding associated with dysentery and menstruation.

See Bolus Armenus for the full entry and source citations.

Carnelian

Carnelian restrains bleeding, especially menstrual and hemorrhoidal bleeding.

See Carnelian for the full entry and source citations.

Haematite

Grind it in water until the liquid turns blood-red, then use it to treat bloody sputum, dysentery, menses, and other fluxes of blood. Crushed and drunk with water, it heals fluxes of the bladder and bowels, menstruation, and bloody saliva.

See Haematite for the full entry and source citations.

Jet

Its odor or smoke provokes menstruation. When washed in water, or when its fumes are applied from below, it brings on menstruation.

Jet is also said to ease childbirth: the steeped draught eases a pregnant womb in travail, and jet ground in water and given to a pregnant woman hastens birth.

See Jet for the full entry and source citations.

Milk and nursing

Crystal

Ground and mixed with honey, or dissolved in honey, it increases the milk of nursing women.

Gryphon — Engraved on crystal. — Fills a woman’s breasts with milk.

See Crystal for the full entry and source citations.

Galactite

Galactite promotes milk in nursing women. Mixed with mead, it is drunk by a nursing mother just after rising from the bath and before eating. Bound around the neck, it fills the breasts with milk. Tied around the thigh during the pains of childbirth, it eases labor. One account specifies a thread of wool from a pregnant sheep.

See Galactite for the full entry and source citations.

Vulture Stone

The stone heals the one who wears it, protects against every kind of misfortune, fills a woman’s breasts with milk, and ensures success for those who ask for it.

See Vulture Stone for the full entry and source citations.

Animal fertility and birth

Topaz

Chrysolite strengthens newborn sheep and other animals when present at birth, helping them move sooner.

See Topaz for the full entry and source citations.
