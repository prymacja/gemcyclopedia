Virtue, Favor, and Prosperity

Wealth, honor, and prosperity

Aetites

Aetites makes its bearer amiable, sober, and rich, shields him from adversity, and grants affection and victory. It preserves honors and healthy offspring.

See Aetites for the full entry and source citations.

Chrysocolla

Another says that it increases wealth.

See Chrysocolla for the full entry and source citations.

Corvina

It increases riches, brings honors, and foretells future events.

See Corvina for the full entry and source citations.

Crystal

Unspecified image — Engraved on crystal or any precious stone, bound in brass, with musk and Amber beneath it, and worn. — Bends both sexes to the bearer’s will, commands spirits, increases his abilities, and brings great wealth.

Lion’s head, two-headed dragon with a small tail, or a man striking the beast with a baton — Engraved on crystal, set in brass, with musk and Amber beneath it. — Makes everyone obey the bearer and increases his wealth.

Unspecified image (the referent is unclear) — Engraved on Kabrates or crystal. — Makes the bearer rich, victorious, and eloquent.

See Crystal for the full entry and source citations.

Emerald

It tempers anger and lust, and makes the wearer gracious to God and men. It increases wealth, sharpens memory, and gives strength to arguments and persuasive speech.

Worn about the neck, it cures hemitertian fever and epilepsy, strengthens weak sight, preserves the eyes, and improves memory. To cure epilepsy, look upon it on nine successive mornings and recite the prescribed prayer.

An image of a merchant carrying goods, or a man seated beneath a centurion, brings wealth, victory, and freedom from distress and evil.

See Emerald for the full entry and source citations.

Heliotrope

Heliotrope gives its bearer a good reputation, honor, health, and long life.

See Heliotrope for the full entry and source citations.

Hyacinth

They guard travelers from noxious air and contagious disease, defend against lightning and enemies, bring honor and wealth, and help secure a favorable hearing from princes.

The cold Hyacinth benefits the body, induces sleep, and brings riches, cleverness, and happiness.

Horse bearing a crocodile: carved on Hyacinth and bound in gold, it gives victory in civil lawsuits and makes its wearer well-mannered, kind, and amiable.

Jupiter enthroned with four men before him, hands raised to heaven and crowned: carved on Hyacinth and set in gold, it grants the wearer’s wishes from wise men and kings.

Crowned youth seated on a throne carried by four men, with hands raised beneath a circle: carved on white Hyacinth and set in a silver ring of equal weight, with mastic and turpentine resin beneath the stone, its wax seal brings the bearer whatever he seeks from kings, powerful people, and wise men of pure mind.

See Hyacinth for the full entry and source citations.

Malachite

It protects children from accidents and hurtful phantasms, so that they may grow up in prosperity.

See Malachite for the full entry and source citations.

Orphanus

Orphanus preserves royal honor.

See Orphanus for the full entry and source citations.

Radaim

Radaim enables its possessor to obtain whatever he wishes. It heals its wearer, brings him honor, and aids him in governing.

See Radaim for the full entry and source citations.

Ruby

It also drives away pestilential and poisonous air, quiets lust, preserves bodily health, banishes evil thoughts, reconciles friends who quarrel, and increases prosperity.

See Ruby for the full entry and source citations.

Spinel

Spinel’s virtue is said to resemble that of Ruby; its entry does not specify which individual powers are shared.

See Spinel for the full entry and source citations.

Vulture Stone

The stone heals the one who wears it, protects against every kind of misfortune, fills a woman’s breasts with milk, and ensures success for those who ask for it.

See Vulture Stone for the full entry and source citations.

Eloquence, grace, and social favor

Agate

All agates strengthen the wearer, give a healthy complexion, eloquence, grace, and favour, and help him overcome danger.

An agate warmed next to the bare skin makes the bearer capable, judicious, and prudent in speech.

An image of a rooster or three young women gives the bearer favor among men and power over spirits of the air, and serves in magical arts.

Cretan black-and-yellow: Overcomes perils, strengthens the body, protects against adversity, and makes the wearer pleasing, persuasive, eloquent, and of good colour.

Cretan coral-like: Sharpens sight, quenches thirst, counters poison, and gives grace, eloquence, preservation, and strength.

See Agate for the full entry and source citations.

Alectorius

It brings honors and preserves them, helps restore lost kingdoms and conquer foreign ones, and gives eloquence, constancy, graciousness, and persuasive power. It makes friends agree, pleases a wife, and increases sexual desire.

See Alectorius for the full entry and source citations.

Cambnites

Worn on the person, Cambnites makes the bearer gracious, affable, and amiable.

See Cambnites for the full entry and source citations.

Celidonius

They grant eloquence and persuasive power, soften the anger of rulers, and refresh weary sight.

See Celidonius for the full entry and source citations.

Galactite

If left there until it melts, it can obliterate memory. When ground three times in water, dried, and drunk with clear water, it reconciles those at odds and joins them in a lasting love.

Carried on the person, Galactite immediately appeases a king’s anger and restores his favor to one who has offended him. It brings victory in legal causes, makes its bearer astute, amiable, and eloquent, and guards against enchantment.

See Galactite for the full entry and source citations.

Garatides

Worn, Garatides makes its bearer pleasant, amiable, and gracious, and reveals what others think of him.

See Garatides for the full entry and source citations.

Hieracites

Black Hieracites shares the virtue of Garatides: it makes its bearer amiable and gracious and reveals what others think of him.

See Hieracites for the full entry and source citations.

Kabrates

Kabrates makes its bearer eloquent, cheerful, honorable, and amiable.

Jupiter — A figure of Jupiter, commonly seated with a baton and globe, or carved as a man with a ram’s head, when engraved on Kabrates, makes its bearer fortunate and amiable, helps him obtain what he desires—especially from clergy—and raises him to dignity and honor.

Eagle — An eagle engraved on Kabrates or Rock Crystal makes its bearer rich, victorious, and eloquent.

See Kabrates for the full entry and source citations.

Magnet

When worn, the magnet reconciles wives and husbands, removes fears and suspicions, and makes a person gracious, persuasive, and elegant in speech. It grants skill in teaching and argument.

See Magnet for the full entry and source citations.

Peace, friendship, and self-command

Alabaster

It gives victory and preserves friendship.

See Alabaster for the full entry and source citations.

Balagius

It overcomes and represses troublesome and lustful thoughts, reconciles quarrels among friends, and brings good health.

See Balagius for the full entry and source citations.

Beryl

It brings mildness of manner and cleverness. Carried, held, and looked upon frequently, it keeps its bearer tranquil and less given to quarrels.

Beryl makes husband and wife agree and strengthens their conjugal love. It brings wealth, honors, and victory over rivals.

When touched, it reconciles enemies and brings friendship to those at odds.

Dog — A leporini dog engraved on beryl brings great honors, fame, and benevolence.

See Beryl for the full entry and source citations.

Carnelian

Another account adds that it calms anger.

See Carnelian for the full entry and source citations.

Chalcedony

Pierced and worn on the neck or hand, it secures success in lawsuits.

Its bearer becomes tranquil, difficult to provoke justly, and safe from unjust harm.

See Chalcedony for the full entry and source citations.

Hephaestite

The water cools and settles into a tranquil pool.

See Hephaestite for the full entry and source citations.

Phengites

Carried in the left hand, red Fongites removes bodily aches and calms anger.

See Phengites for the full entry and source citations.

Sardachat

Sardachat is associated with the name “peace-making” through a passage linking it to Agate.

See Sardachat for the full entry and source citations.

Wisdom, knowledge, memory, and judgment

Amandinus

It also grants understanding of prophecy and the interpretation of dreams and riddles, enabling the interpreter to solve the questions set before him.

See Amandinus for the full entry and source citations.

Amethyst

It keeps the bearer awake at night, represses evil thoughts, gives good understanding of knowable things, and makes him skilled and expert in his actions.

An armed man with a sword engraved on Sardion or amethyst gives the wearer a good and perfect memory and makes him wise.

See Amethyst for the full entry and source citations.

Exacolitus

Exacolitus has a solvent or dissolving virtue, as learned physicians teach.

See Exacolitus for the full entry and source citations.

Sapphire

Moisten it in the mouth, warm it over wine vapour, and swallow its moisture to improve understanding and knowledge and help the stomach. Rub it upon the tongue while fasting to improve understanding, provided the bearer intends no malice.

The stone confirms the mind in goodness and brings peaceful agreements.

An astrolabe engraved on sapphire increases wealth and foretells things to come.

A man engraved as if playing a musical instrument raises the bearer to greater honours and makes him gracious to all.

See Sapphire for the full entry and source citations.

Sardonyx

To strengthen understanding, knowledge, and all the bodily senses, place the stone against bare skin and often bring it to the mouth so that the breath passes over it during inhalation and exhalation.

Some sources say it removes lustful inclinations and makes a person chaste, modest, amiable, and merry.

See Sardonyx for the full entry and source citations.

Topaz

Set in gold, it dispels phantasms, expels stupidity, and gives wisdom.

Carried always, it strengthens knowledge. Placed over the heart, it preserves knowledge and skill.

Set in gold, the Ethiopian Crisolitus drives away terrors of the night, gives wisdom and honors, and dispels folly.

For protection, place Topaz over the heart each morning and say: “May God, who is magnified above all things and in all ways, not reject me from his honor, but may he preserve, strengthen, and establish me with his blessing.” Evil will abhor the bearer, and the stone deflects assaults.

It increases wealth and drives away anger and sorrow. Its bearer gains a prince’s favor.

See Topaz for the full entry and source citations.

Joy, health, and well-being

Chrysoprase

It aids sight, makes its bearer diligent in good works, banishes avarice, gladdens the heart, and removes strong passions.

See Chrysoprase for the full entry and source citations.

Filaterius

It induces cheerfulness and wisdom, makes its wearer well-behaved, and comforts the spirits.

See Filaterius for the full entry and source citations.

Garnet

Garnet gladdens the heart and dispels sorrow.

See Garnet for the full entry and source citations.

Jasper

It calms disorders of the humors in the heart, kidneys, or elsewhere.

See Jasper for the full entry and source citations.

Phrygius

It calms swollen flesh around a wound, heals growing and malignant ulcers, and stops the flux of the eyes.

See Phrygius for the full entry and source citations.

Samius

Taken in drink, Samius cures dizziness, calms the mind, and prevents miscarriage.

See Samius for the full entry and source citations.

Sard

It gladdens the spirit and sharpens the wits. It prevents terrible dreams, increases wealth and cheerfulness, restrains bleeding, and grants victory over enemies.

For severe head illness, tie it atop the head in a hat, cloth, or leather pouch and recite the accompanying prayer asking God to cut away the illness and restore sound understanding.

See Sard for the full entry and source citations.

Good fortune and fulfillment

Cimedia

Cimedia foretells calm or stormy weather in the air and at sea.

See Cimedia for the full entry and source citations.

Cinaedia

Cinaedia foretells calm or storm at sea.

See Cinaedia for the full entry and source citations.

Coral

It also gives a happy beginning and successful end to undertakings.

See Coral for the full entry and source citations.

Hexacontalite

Hexacontalite is said to bear as many virtues as the colors and gems it contains; no single effect is specified.

See Hexacontalite for the full entry and source citations.

Pantherus

If one looks upon the stone early in the morning as the sun rises, success and victory follow.

See Pantherus for the full entry and source citations.
