Necromancy

This page groups gem powers involving the summoning, questioning, and commanding of spirits, along with other practices described as necromancy.

Index

  • Summoning and commanding spirits (9 gems)
  • Necromancy and spirit-mediated divination (3 gems)

Summoning and commanding spirits

Agate

An image of a rooster or three young women gives the bearer power over spirits of the air.

See Agate for the full entry and source citations.

Anancitis

During hydromancy, Anancitis calls out the images of demons. It also calls forth demons and spirits.

See Anancitis for the full entry and source citations.

Beryl

The image calls forth water spirits, compels them to speak, and invokes the dead to answer questions.

See Beryl for the full entry and source citations.

Diacodos

Diacodos is so effective in calling up phantasms that magicians make much use of it. When it is cast into water while the proper charm is spoken, various demons appear and answer questions. Held in the mouth, it summons any demon from hell, which then gives answers to questions put to it. Held in the hand, it commands spirits and makes the shapes of summoned demons appear within its orb. It may be used to call water spirits and reveal their forms.

See Diacodos for the full entry and source citations.

Galactite

It compels summoned spirits to answer.

See Galactite for the full entry and source citations.

Pontica

By Pontica one may question demons.

See Pontica for the full entry and source citations.

Synochitis

Synochitis is used to control spirits summoned from below.

See Synochitis for the full entry and source citations.

Amethyst

A man holding a knife and seated over a dragon, engraved on amethyst set in a lead or iron ring, compels spirits to obey, reveals treasures, and answers questions.

See Amethyst for the full entry and source citations.

Crystal

Unspecified image — Engraved on crystal or any precious stone, bound in brass, with musk and Amber beneath it, and worn. — Bends both sexes to the bearer’s will, commands spirits, increases his abilities, and brings great wealth.

See Crystal for the full entry and source citations.

Necromancy and spirit-mediated divination

Heliotrope

Consecrated with an incantation and magical signs, Heliotrope makes those possessed speak prophetic words.

Sapphire

The sapphire has great efficacy in necromancy.

See Sapphire for the full entry and source citations.

Magnet

The magnet is said to call up phantoms when used with incantations and magical signs.

See Magnet for the full entry and source citations.
