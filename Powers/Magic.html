Magical arts, rites, and engraved figures

Agate

An image of a rooster or three young women brings its bearer favor among people. Agate also serves in magical arts.

See Agate for the full entry and source citations.

When set in iron, Calorites is useful in the magical arts.

Garamantica is useful in the arts of magic.

Glossopetra

Haematite

Magicians use Haematite to discover the ambushes of barbarians.

See Haematite for the full entry and source citations.

Galactite

It makes magical writings intelligible.

See Galactite for the full entry and source citations.

Kacamon

Its powers are said to arise from its engraved images and sigils, which the source treats separately.

Influence, foresight, and hidden powers

Heliotrope

Magicians also consecrate Heliotrope with verses and inscribe characters upon it to grant its owner foresight. Joined with the juice of its namesake herb and the proper incantation, it hides its bearer from mortal sight.

See Heliotrope for the full entry and source citations.

Filaterius

It induces cheerfulness and wisdom, makes its wearer well-behaved, and comforts the spirits.

See Filaterius for the full entry and source citations.

Pantherus

It is said to possess as many powers as it has colors.

See Pantherus for the full entry and source citations.

Radaim

Radaim enables its possessor to obtain whatever he wishes.

See Radaim for the full entry and source citations.

Sard

It gladdens the wearer and sharpens the wits.

See Sard for the full entry and source citations.

Jasper

Incantations recited over Jasper make its bearer pleasing, powerful, and safe.

See Jasper for the full entry and source citations.

Magnet

The magnet is also said to make a great iron cult-statue hang in a temple as though it floated in the air.

See Magnet for the full entry and source citations.
