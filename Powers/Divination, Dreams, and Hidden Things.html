Dreams, sleep, and night visions

Agate

When the black-and-white kind is placed beneath a sleeper's head, it brings many dreams.

Black-and-white: Dreams when placed beneath a sleeper's head.

See Agate for the full entry and source citations.

Ceranius

It grants victory in legal cases and battles and makes dreams sweet and pleasant.

See Ceranius for the full entry and source citations.

Crystal

Hung over a sleeper, it dispels bad dreams and frees those who have been charmed.

For blurred vision, warm crystal in the sun and place it often over the eyes.

See Crystal for the full entry and source citations.

Diamond

Worn on the left arm, it gives victory and courage and guards against enemies, wild beasts, savage men, weapons, quarrels, phantoms, and night terrors.

See Diamond for the full entry and source citations.

Eumetis

When placed beneath the head during sleep, Eumetis turns dreams into oracles.

See Eumetis for the full entry and source citations.

Hyacinth

The stone also eases childbirth and drives away nightmares.

Woman above, fish below, holding a mirror and branch: set in a gold ring on the finger, the wearer turns the stone toward the palm and closes the hand to become invisible.

Mermaid with a mirror and branch on sea-Hyacinth: set in a gold ring and covered with a wax seal, it makes the bearer invisible when the stone is held tightly in the palm.

Touching a woman with it makes her obey, and placing it beneath the pillow brings desired dreams.

Placed beneath the head, it brings desired dreams.

See Hyacinth for the full entry and source citations.

Jasper

Carrying Jasper disperses phantasms and threats that appear in dreams, especially thunder and lightning.

Worn at the neck, Jasper eases labor, guards against misfortune and night phantoms, and gains strength from blessing and silver.

Remedies fever phantasms.

See Jasper for the full entry and source citations.

Onyx

When worn around the neck or on a finger, Onyx brings sorrow and fear, frightful dreams, and quarrels or lawsuits.

See Onyx for the full entry and source citations.

Sard

It prevents terrible dreams, increases wealth and cheerfulness, restrains bleeding, and grants victory over enemies.

See Sard for the full entry and source citations.

Topaz

Set in gold, it dispels phantasms, expels stupidity, and gives wisdom.

Set in gold and carried in the left hand, Crisoletus drives away demons, nightmares, and melancholy visions.

To clarify obscured vision, soak Topaz in pure wine for three days and nights, then moisten the stone in the wine and rub it around the eyes at night, letting a little liquid touch the inside of the eyes.

See Topaz for the full entry and source citations.

Foretelling events and reading signs

Amandinus

It also grants understanding of prophecy and the interpretation of dreams and riddles, enabling the interpreter to solve the questions set before him.

See Amandinus for the full entry and source citations.

Amethyst

The stone foretells southern winds, winter weather, and rainfall.

See Amethyst for the full entry and source citations.

Celontes

Held under the tongue, Celontes foretells future events, but its virtue is bound to the moon.

See Celontes for the full entry and source citations.

Chelonites

Held beneath the tongue, Chelonites allows one to foretell the future.

See Chelonites for the full entry and source citations.

Cimedia

Cimedia foretells calm or stormy weather in the air and at sea.

See Cimedia for the full entry and source citations.

Cinaedia

Cinaedia foretells calm or storm at sea.

See Cinaedia for the full entry and source citations.

Corvina

It increases riches, brings honors, and foretells future events.

See Corvina for the full entry and source citations.

Emerald

It gives foresight, aids divination, and aids the foreknowledge of cases to be pleaded.

See Emerald for the full entry and source citations.

Hiena

Wash the mouth, then place the stone beneath the tongue: it grants the power of foretelling the future.

See Hiena for the full entry and source citations.

Lignites

It cures folly and grants the ability to foretell the future.

See Lignites for the full entry and source citations.

Limphicus

This protection extends into the future as well as the present.

See Limphicus for the full entry and source citations.

Sapphire

For red and inflamed eyes or obscured vision, moisten the stone with saliva in the mouth and apply the saliva around and into the eyes.

It frees prisoners, promotes concord, and favours prayer and divination.

An astrolabe engraved on sapphire increases wealth and foretells things to come.

See Sapphire for the full entry and source citations.

Silenite

Held beneath the tongue at appointed hours of the waxing moon, Silonites foretells future events; its virtue wanes with the moon.

See Silenite for the full entry and source citations.

Revealing thoughts and hidden things

Amber

Placed upon a wife's left breast while she sleeps, amber is said to make her confess her misdeeds.

See Amber for the full entry and source citations.

Garatides

Worn, Garatides makes its bearer pleasant, amiable, and gracious, and reveals what others think of him. Held in the mouth, it enables him to pass correct judgments and discern well between differing opinions.

See Garatides for the full entry and source citations.

Geranites

Rinse Geranites and place it in the mouth to know what others think of its bearer.

See Geranites for the full entry and source citations.

Hieracites

Black Hieracites shares the power of Garatides to reveal what others think of its bearer.

See Hieracites for the full entry and source citations.

Obtalius

When carried with a laurel leaf and the proper charms, it sharpens the wearer’s sight and darkens the sight of those around, until they are unable to see.

See Obtalius for the full entry and source citations.

Ophthalmus

It dims the sight of those nearby and makes the wearer seem invisible.

See Ophthalmus for the full entry and source citations.

Quirita

Placed on the breast of a sleeper, Quirita reveals secrets and produces hallucinations.

See Quirita for the full entry and source citations.

Quirus

Placed on the chest of a sleeping person, Quirus compels him to disclose all his misdeeds.

See Quirus for the full entry and source citations.

Yectios

Some use Yectios as a touchstone, or paragon, for discerning metals.

See Yectios for the full entry and source citations.

Summoning spirits and producing visions

Beryl

Its wearer becomes solicitous and is freed from idle dreams when the stone is hung about the neck.

The image calls forth water spirits, compels them to speak, and invokes the dead to answer questions.

See Beryl for the full entry and source citations.

Coral

It drives away ghosts, demons, illusions, and nightmares, restrains lightning, winds, tempests, and whirlwinds, and protects against wild beasts.

See Coral for the full entry and source citations.

Diacodos

Diacodos is so effective in calling up phantasms that magicians make much use of it. When it is cast into water while the proper charm is spoken, various demons appear and answer questions. Held in the mouth, it summons any demon from hell, which then gives answers to questions put to it.

See Diacodos for the full entry and source citations.

Hamonis

When a person is in contemplation, Hamonis shows the mind all sorts of divine things.

See Hamonis for the full entry and source citations.

Jet

Jet is used against dropsy, stomach and belly disorders, and melancholy’s phantasms.

See Jet for the full entry and source citations.

Magnet

Moistened with the saliva of one troubled by madness or phantasms, then rubbed over the forehead and crown while the prescribed prayer is spoken, the stone restores the sufferer’s senses.

Hidden beneath a sleeping wife’s head, it makes a chaste wife embrace her husband, while an unchaste wife is thrown from the bed in terror.

The magnet is said to call up phantoms when used with incantations and magic signs, and to hang a great iron cult-statue in a temple as though it floated in the air.

See Magnet for the full entry and source citations.

Malachite

It protects children from accidents and hurtful phantasms, so that they may grow up in prosperity.

See Malachite for the full entry and source citations.

Ruby

Airy spirits cannot complete their phantasms where the stone is present.

Its radiance remains visible through many layers of clothing, so it cannot be hidden.

See Ruby for the full entry and source citations.
