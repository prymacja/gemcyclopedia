Invisibility and Concealment

This page groups the explicit powers assigned to gemstones in their linked articles. Each gemstone section links back to its article, where the source passages and citations are retained.

Hiding the wearer from sight

Heliotrope

Joined with the juice of its namesake herb and the proper incantation, it hides its bearer from mortal sight.

See Heliotrope for the full entry and source citations.

Hyacinth

Woman above, fish below, holding a mirror and branch: set in a gold ring on the finger, the wearer turns the stone toward the palm and closes the hand to become invisible.

Mermaid with a mirror and branch on sea-Hyacinth: set in a gold ring and covered with a wax seal, it makes the bearer invisible when the stone is held tightly in the palm.

See Hyacinth for the full entry and source citations.

Ophthalmus

It dims the sight of those nearby and makes the wearer seem invisible.

See Ophthalmus for the full entry and source citations.

Ostolanus

Ostolanus makes its bearer invisible while leaving the bearer’s own sight unimpaired.

See Ostolanus for the full entry and source citations.

Obscuring the sight of others

Obtalius

When carried with a laurel leaf and the proper charms, it sharpens the wearer’s sight and darkens the sight of those around, until they are unable to see.

See Obtalius for the full entry and source citations.

Opal

Opal keeps the bearer’s eyes clear while clouding and blinding nearby observers.

See Opal for the full entry and source citations.

Ostolanus

Ostolanus draws a cloud over the eyes of nearby people while preserving the bearer’s sight.

See Ostolanus for the full entry and source citations.

Concealing actions and theft

Ophthalmius

It clouds all other eyes, so that a thief may carry away plunder openly and securely.

See Ophthalmius for the full entry and source citations.
